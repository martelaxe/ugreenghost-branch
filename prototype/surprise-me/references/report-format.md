# Discovery report format

Present at most three findings, ranked by practical usefulness, evidence strength, feasibility and surprise. Use only information actually returned by completed experts; distinguish source facts from hypotheses and label uncertainty.

## Discovery

State the connection and its classification: `SUPPORTED`, `NEEDS VERIFICATION`, `BLOCKED`, `REJECTED`, or `INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE`. A novel combination is a hypothesis, not an established benefit. Preserve a candidate as unverified when review is unavailable and sufficient retrieved context exists; otherwise report insufficient evidence. Do not invent a candidate when none is supported by the available context.

## Why it matters

Explain the possible practical benefit conditionally unless established by source evidence or user confirmation.

## Evidence for and against

For each material claim, list actual file paths or stable identifiers and excerpts when returned. Distinguish supporting, contradicting and merely related information, and state whether it was retrieved. Say when references or excerpts are missing; matching text alone does not prove semantic support.

## Blockers and uncertainty

Identify critical restrictions and cite their actual source where available. If a reported blocker, permission or safety condition is unverified, uncited or inaccessible, say so and do not recommend the dependent action. Describe missing facts, unresolved assumptions and limits of the review.

## Recommended next action

Give one safe, proportionate step and identify any required user or platform approval. Never perform the action as part of this report.

## Investigation coverage

State the authorized scope and effective limits; report discovered, selected and read counts only when available. Identify known skipped, missing, unreadable or incompletely processed files. A reached limit, partial result or missing completeness information means coverage is incomplete or unknown. Do not assume pagination or unseen results.

## Optional suggested groupings

Offer a short textual grouping only if useful and supported by returned files. Do not move or rename anything.
