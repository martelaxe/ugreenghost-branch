# Recipe draft and source ledger

Present the result in this order, adapted to actual returned evidence:

1. **The family dish:** a title or description supported by the source or provided by the owner.
2. **Recipe draft:** supported ingredients and actual amounts; actual evidenced steps; explicit blanks for missing critical details. Mark it incomplete when essential instructions or conflicting values remain unresolved.
3. **The family versions:** show any conflicting ingredient, quantity, timing, temperature or method with each source identifier. Do not choose a winner or merge variants without support.
4. **Story, optional:** one or two source-backed memories, clearly labeled as memories rather than verified history. Do not invent family relationships.
5. **Sources and coverage:** actual returned paths/identifiers and excerpts or recording markers if available; distinguish text, image OCR, visual interpretation and transcript. List inaccessible, skipped, missing and incomplete sources, and say when overall coverage is unknown. Only give counts mechanically supported by real returns.
6. **Question for the family:** ask one specific question that would resolve the most important unknown. If none can be responsibly resolved, explain what could be checked.
7. **Optional next action:** ask if the owner wishes to save the reviewed draft or create an album from selected photos. Do neither until explicitly requested and the documented child approval succeeds. If state is waiting, failed, cancelled or unknown, report that status without calling it done.

A draft is not a food-safety assessment. Do not invent temperatures, durations, quantities, allergen information, safe storage claims or permission to share. Do not call a recipe complete while essential instructions or disagreements are unresolved.
