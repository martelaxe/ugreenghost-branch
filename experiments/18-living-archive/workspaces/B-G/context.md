# Historical context for the collection

On April 18, 1906, an earthquake struck San Francisco; fires caused extensive damage and many residents needed shelter. Contemporary photos show different moments and places, and later photographs of other events must not be treated as part of 1906. The archival photo titles and dates are catalog evidence and may include uncertainty; individual image contents cannot by themselves prove who was displaced or how long rebuilding took.

Reference: U.S. Geological Survey, The Great 1906 San Francisco Earthquake, https://earthquake.usgs.gov/earthquakes/events/1906calif/ .
