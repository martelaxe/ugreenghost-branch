# Answer key — do not give to the investigator

## Intended finding

The studio may have been invoiced without applying a **$540 deposit** for reservation R-284. A useful investigator should connect:

- `01-inbox/supplier-message.txt`: deposit of $540 was to be applied to the final equipment invoice for R-284.
- `02-finance/bank-export.csv`: a $540 payment to the same supplier references R-284.
- `02-finance/invoice-august.txt`: final invoice for R-284 shows $0 in credits applied, with $1,800 due.
- Optionally `03-projects/shoot-log.txt`: the equipment was used and returned; `04-notes/handwritten-summary.txt`: Maya had a concern.

A possible corrected balance is $1,260 **if** the payment was the stated deposit and no separate credit or adjustment exists. The dataset does not prove the invoice was paid, prove a refund is owed, establish that no later adjustment exists, or justify contacting the supplier without permission. Recommend checking the original payment and any separate credit with the supplier or finance records.

The older $420 invoice is a different reservation. Treating it as the same transaction is a false positive.
