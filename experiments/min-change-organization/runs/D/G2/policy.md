USER POLICY (this is a plan, not permission to make changes)

Consider every regular file returned under the selected archive. A record's category is the unique value in its `Category:` field (finance, projects, or personal); its year is the unique four-digit year in `Date:` fields. A file whose readable contents lack these fields, contain conflicting values/years, or cannot be read must be deferred and explained. Names and extensions are not trustworthy classification evidence. All files in this archive are records regardless of extension. Never delete or merge duplicates.

A record satisfies the requested organization if its direct parent is either `sorted/CATEGORY/YEAR` or `historic/CATEGORY/YEAR`; both are equally acceptable. Leave a compliant file where it is. For a confident noncompliant record, propose moving it without renaming to either acceptable directory. Do not propose moving to a path occupied at inventory time, even if another move might later vacate it. Never overwrite; only propose destinations inside the archive. If neither alternative is free, defer and mention the collision. If multiple noncompliant records compete for too few currently free acceptable paths with the same target basename, defer all of that competing group and ask me to decide; do not choose a winner or rename them. When multiple safe destinations are free, either is acceptable. No mutation is approved.
