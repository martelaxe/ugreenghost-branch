The workshop notes say movable bands make changes easy to notice, and the materials record says the prototype used white card, blue offcuts, and scissors ([demo notes](<archive/01-projects/folded-water/demo-notes.txt>), [materials](<archive/01-projects/folded-water/materials.txt>)). The pattern description specifies three slits, though it is a verbal description and there is no finished stencil ([shape diagram](<archive/01-projects/folded-water/shape-diagram.txt>)). The old-copy note describes two slits and does not supply authoritative signoff, so use the later three-slit description as the best available guide while treating the design as something to confirm with the makers ([old copy](<archive/02-assets/notes/old-copy.txt>)).

   **Rights and limits:** Mara and the collaborator authorize the described pattern for a free community educational display, with credit to both makers; commercial resale is excluded ([Folded Water rights](<archive/03-rights/folded-water.txt>)). This supports the narrow proposed use. Do not add the indexed photos or the S-3 recording: photo terms require separate written consent for public display and none is recorded, while recording permission and ownership are unconfirmed ([photo terms](<archive/03-rights/photo-terms.txt>), [sound terms](<archive/03-rights/sound-terms.txt>)).

   **Next steps:** Check whether the listed card, scraps, scissors, and tape are still available ([craft box](<archive/02-assets/notes/craft-box.txt>)); make a quick paper mock-up; confirm the three-slit pattern with both makers if practical; and ask library staff to confirm a date before preparing the table ([venue](<archive/04-resources/venue.txt>)). The materials check is dated October 2, and the display is not booked.

3. **Hypothesis: combine the two projects as a “changing shoreline” table**

   A single free educational table could pair the FW-7 movable bands with Tide Lines captions and observations: the paper model gives visitors something to manipulate, while the notes provide a local example. The combination is grounded in the Folded Water workshop’s changing-shoreline activity and Tide Lines’ changing-water-level theme ([workshop](<archive/01-projects/folded-water/workshop.txt>), [concept](<archive/01-projects/tide-lines/concept.txt>)).

   **Why this remains unresolved:** The two rights records separately support the proposed narrow uses, but do not explicitly address combining or adapting the materials together. Keep each contribution identifiable, credit both Folded Water makers and Mara, and confirm that the makers are comfortable with the combined presentation before using it. Do not treat the combination as permission to include third-party photos or sound.

   **Next steps:** Sketch the layout using only the described pattern and Mara’s permitted writing, then seek confirmation from the makers and the library before producing it.
