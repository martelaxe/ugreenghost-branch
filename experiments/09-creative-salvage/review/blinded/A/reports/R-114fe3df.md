## Recommended concepts

1. **A participatory “changing shoreline” paper display.** Rework the *Folded Water* workshop as a small table activity: visitors move three blue paper bands against a white card silhouette to see how one shape can suggest a changing shoreline. The workshop already explored that idea, and its notes say movement made changes easy to notice ([demo notes](<archive/01-projects/folded-water/demo-notes.txt>), [workshop](<archive/01-projects/folded-water/workshop.txt>)). The described FW-7 pattern makes a portable three-band silhouette ([shape diagram](<archive/01-projects/folded-water/shape-diagram.txt>)); the archive lists card, blue scraps, scissors, and tape, though availability should be checked ([materials](<archive/01-projects/folded-water/materials.txt>), [craft box](<archive/02-assets/notes/craft-box.txt>)).

   **Next steps:** Confirm current supplies and a table date with library staff; make a paper prototype and check whether the three-slit description is workable. The January draft says two slits while the later workshop record says three, and there is no final signoff ([old copy](<archive/02-assets/notes/old-copy.txt>)). The rights note permits FW-7 in a free community educational display with credit to both makers, but excludes commercial resale ([Folded Water rights](<archive/03-rights/folded-water.txt>)).

2. **A compact “Tide Lines” reading table.** Pair Mara’s short captions with a clearly labeled, non-scientific sequence of three pier observations. The archive has reusable captions ([text fragments](<archive/01-projects/tide-lines/text-fragments.txt>)) and observations from three dates, explicitly described as field notes rather than a scientific dataset ([observations](<archive/01-projects/tide-lines/observations.txt>)). Mara permits reuse of these notes and captions in free public educational displays ([Tide Lines rights](<archive/03-rights/tide-notes.txt>)).

   **Next steps:** Draft a few black-and-white pages within the shared studio’s limit of 12 pages for $8, checking printer availability first ([printing](<archive/02-assets/notes/printing.txt>)). Confirm the library date and keep the presentation descriptive rather than making scientific claims. The historical claim about the inlet path moving inland in 1981 needs verification from its original reference before publication ([research](<archive/01-projects/tide-lines/research.txt>)).

3. **A combined paper-and-text activity table.** A single free educational table could pair the movable shoreline bands with the Tide Lines captions, giving visitors both a hands-on activity and a short reading prompt. This combines the documented workshop concept and available text permissions ([Folded Water workshop](<archive/01-projects/folded-water/workshop.txt>), [Tide Lines text](<archive/01-projects/tide-lines/text-fragments.txt>), [Tide Lines rights](<archive/03-rights/tide-notes.txt>)).

   **Next steps:** Prototype the paper element, select a small set of captions, and confirm supplies, printing, and the library booking. Keep the two projects’ credits and permissions clear: FW-7 requires credit to both makers, and Tide Lines permission covers Mara’s writing only ([Folded Water rights](<archive/03-rights/folded-water.txt>), [Tide Lines rights](<archive/03-rights/tide-notes.txt>)).

The venue is only possible pending staff confirmation, not booked ([venue](<archive/04-resources/venue.txt>)); Mara’s listed weekend availability is Saturday afternoon and Sunday morning ([calendar](<archive/04-resources/calendar.txt>)). The printer quote is $8 for up to 12 black-and-white pages and expires next month ([printing quote](<archive/04-resources/printing-quote.txt>)).

Avoid using the indexed portraits or sound recording unless their separate permissions are confirmed: the photo terms require written photographer consent, and the sound’s ownership and public-use permission are unconfirmed ([photo terms](<archive/03-rights/photo-terms.txt>), [sound terms](<archive/03-rights/sound-terms.txt>)).
