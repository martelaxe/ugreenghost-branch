Ela’s apple loaf can be partially recovered from the family materials:

- 2 cups flour
- 2 apples, chopped
- 2 eggs
- 1 cup milk, according to the [recipe card](/tmp/ugreen-recipe-qnw7ebe5/cards/apple-loaf-card.png)
- Mix and bake.

The [2018 gathering notebook](/tmp/ugreen-recipe-qnw7ebe5/notes/gathering.md) supplies the eggs and says to chop the apples. A [2019 family conversation transcript](/tmp/ugreen-recipe-qnw7ebe5/memories/voice-transcript.txt) recalls using **1 cup water instead of milk** for the autumn fair loaf. That may describe a variation; the materials don’t establish which liquid Ela normally used.

Before using the recipe, check the **oven temperature and baking time**: neither the card nor the notebook gives them. Also check whether the ingredient list is complete, including whether it needs a raising agent, sugar, fat or salt, and what size loaf pan Ela used. The surviving notes don’t answer those questions, so the result is uncertain as written.

The ginger mentioned in [another note](/tmp/ugreen-recipe-qnw7ebe5/notes/not-the-loaf.md) belongs to an unrelated apple tea and is not evidence for the loaf.
