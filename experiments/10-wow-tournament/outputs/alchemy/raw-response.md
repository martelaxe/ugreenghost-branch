## Six-panel mini-zine — copy-ready text

### PANEL 1 — RIVER, PAPER, POSSIBILITY
**New creative writing**

An unfinished project can become a place to begin.

Read a river observation. Meet a paper experiment. Then imagine what you might make from something left unfinished.

### PANEL 2 — A MOMENT RECORDED
**Source material: Ada’s written observation**

“On Tuesday the river was low enough to reveal the third step. At dusk a paper boat turned beneath the bridge.”

Source: `01-work/river-notebook.txt`. Attribution to Ada and permission for reuse: `03-rights/own-notes.txt`.

**Evidence limit:** This is a personal field-note account. The archive gives no calendar date or river location and provides no independent verification. `01-work/river-notebook.txt`

### PANEL 3 — AN UNFINISHED EXPERIMENT
**Source material: workshop account**

The abandoned paper workshop used three cut-paper waves. Its notes describe blue scraps and white card as making each wave easy to move. `02-old/stencil-notes.txt`

**New creative writing**

Imagine those three waves as three voices:

“I began.”
“I paused.”
“I could begin again.”

These voices are invented for this zine.

### PANEL 4 — THREE WAVES
**New creative writing — an imagined scene, not a historical account**

The first wave folds a little silence.

The second carries an unfinished afternoon.

The third waits at the edge of the page.

Nothing moves until a reader lends it a breath.

### PANEL 5 — YOUR TURN
**New creative writing — an educational activity**

Choose one phrase from the field note.

Write two short lines:

“The note records…”

“I imagine…”

Keep the observation in the first line and your invention in the second.

Then give an unfinished project a new title. What could you carry forward from it?
