From: West Estuary Reading Room, coordinator Lian
Date: 5 October 2026
Our local history group includes several people with low vision. The large print harbor handout is still difficult to follow in a group, especially when discussing how the old piers related to one another. We have not commissioned new materials; we would be interested in hearing about low-cost ways to make a tabletop history session easier to follow. Please do not treat historic diagrams as current wayfinding.
