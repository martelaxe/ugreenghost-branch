# Product differentiation assessment

The experiment gives evidence of one useful synthetic recombination in local Codex and responsible treatment of a documented equipment blocker. It does not demonstrate a NAS implementation, autonomous monitoring, memory, invention in open-ended real archives, a knowledge graph or user value in deployment.

Personal Knowledge Graph** | Helps explore relationships across files. | Visual links can be appealing, but novelty depends on what they reveal. | Entity extraction might be orchestrated; persistent graph creation and visualization are not documented. ## Recommendation

Lead with **C: evidence-grounded opportunity discovery**, phrased carefully: “Discover a possible next step from your files, with the sources and constraints that support it.” Use the investigator framing as a user experience, and treat “invention” as a research direction rather than a guaranteed capability.

The differentiating interaction could be an explicit **“Surprise me” investigation** over an authorized folder, yielding a small evidence card: the idea, connected sources, important blocker, uncertainty and an approval-aware next step. A denied or unverified opportunity should be shown as a hypothesis, not a cleared recommendation. The important product question is whether UGREEN's agents and model can reliably produce that card and its source references; Experiment 03 does not answer it.

Keep organization proposals and file history as later possibilities, subject to catalog and runtime verification. No actual graph, background agent or storage time machine is established.
