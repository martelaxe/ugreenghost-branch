## 1. Raised tabletop harbor-history discussion aid

### Proposed combination

Create a small internal sample using selected features from Harbour Works’ historic schematic WE-98. Combine raised acrylic shapes with short, high-contrast labels and a spoken introduction explaining the historic spatial relationships.

The unexpected connection is between an old display’s coordinate export, a retained layered-acrylic technique, currently usable equipment and stock, and a reading room’s difficulty discussing the relationships between former piers. This would explore a different way to support the discussion, beyond enlarging the existing handout.

### Facts versus hypotheses

**Established facts:** There is a recent, specific discussion-access need; WE-98 has an expressly permitted prototype use; a relevant fabrication technique has been demonstrated; equipment and stock are recorded as available; management has defined a conditional internal-prototype allowance.

**Hypothesis:** A simplified raised diagram, paired with visual labels and spoken orientation, could help some participants follow historic spatial relationships. Neither the acrylic technique nor this particular application has been validated with the intended group.

The coordinate export alone does not establish the geometry needed to explain how the piers related. The underlying WE-98 drawing must be inspected before choosing features or deriving outlines.

### Rights, restrictions, and safety

Attribute the derivative to **Harbour Works** and clearly label it as a **schematic of historic 1998 features, for history discussion only—not current wayfinding**.

The documented rights cover internal prototypes and presentation of proposed educational materials. Confirm authorization before broader distribution or delivery.

Do not add the donated chart M-41: `03-rights/survey-donation.txt` restricts it to preservation and private reference; copying, display, or adaptation needs separate written permission. Portraits and historic recordings also cannot be presumed reusable: see `03-rights/photograph-checklist.md` and `07-old-programmes/audio-consent.txt`.

Fabrication requires extraction, a trained operator, and a current service check under `04-equipment/fume-guidance.md`. PVC vinyl and unknown plastics are prohibited. Inspect the assembled sample for handling hazards before inviting anyone to touch it.
