# Archive opportunity report

**Evaluation date: October 26, 2026.** I found **one sufficiently supported opportunity**. The archive supports a bounded internal prototype, but does not yet establish feasibility for a participant pilot or public release.

## 1. A tactile tabletop model for discussing the historic harbor

Combine the staff-owned 1998 schematic and its coordinate export with approved acrylic offcuts, an existing layered-acrylic technique, and retained accessibility observations. The result could be a small raised model that helps a history group discuss the relationships between former harbor features, accompanied by large, high-contrast labels and spoken orientation.

The useful new combination is **spatial history interpretation through touch and discussion**. The reading room has expressed a difficulty, but has neither requested this particular solution nor commissioned materials. The earlier acrylic demonstration was not a map or accessibility aid.

### Facts versus hypotheses

**Established:** there is a recent, specific access difficulty; a historical schematic with derivative permissions; recorded suitable material and equipment; a relevant fabrication technique; and a conditional allowance for a small internal prototype.

**Hypothesis:** a raised representation could make the relationships between historic features easier for some group members to discuss. Neither the access notes nor the fabrication demonstration validates that benefit. High contrast, texture choices, scale, labels, and spoken explanation need review with intended users.

### Feasibility, rights, and blockers

**Feasibility is plausible for a small internal sample; unproven for a complete usable model.** Four listed coordinates allow a deliberately limited scope. However, the archive does not establish offcut dimensions, current unallocated quantities, assembly supplies, a workable attachment method, or achievable production time.

The original WE-98 drawing is listed as being on the archive drive in `02-archive/chart-register.csv`; it is not supplied among the workspace files. Its geometry and correspondence with the coordinate export therefore remain unverified.

Rights support internal prototyping and presentation of a proposed educational derivative with Harbour Works attribution. They do not establish unrestricted distribution or sale. Keep the proposed sample within that documented scope and label it clearly as a schematic of **1998 historic features, not current wayfinding**.

Current blockers are:

- Manager approval and allocation of staff and operator time.
- Confirmation that suitable offcuts remain available and that assembly can be completed safely within the allowance.
- Inspection of the original drawing and verification of the coordinate interpretation.
- Reading-room interest in this particular approach and voluntary participant involvement.

November availability is conditional: `06-operations/2026-rota.txt` expressly says the September rota does not establish November availability. Existing work must also be protected; `08-admin/weekly-note.txt` prioritizes completing the frame order that week.

### Safe next steps and approvals

1. **Prepare a bounded internal prototype brief for manager approval:** one small sample, using only existing approved stock, with total staff time capped at four hours. Include preparation, cutting, assembly, and inspection within that cap.
2. **Before fabrication, verify the source and materials:** inspect WE-98 through authorized archive access, check the coordinates, measure available offcuts, and confirm the assembly method. Reduce scope or stop if it needs unapproved purchases or additional time.
3. **After approval, make and inspect the sample:** use a trained operator, extraction, and the current service record. Check edges, stability, and any detachable parts before handling. Include attribution and the historical/non-navigation label.
4. **Present the proposal to the reading-room coordinator:** ask whether a voluntary sample review would be useful. Agree participant arrangements before any external trial. Treat feedback as exploratory; do not describe the object as a validated accessibility aid.

No spending, external pilot, delivery commitment, or public release is justified by the current record.
