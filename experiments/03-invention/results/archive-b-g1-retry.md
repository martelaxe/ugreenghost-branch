# Archive opportunity report

**Evaluation date:** October 26, 2026  
**Scope:** All 60 files in the workspace were examined. No evidence was modified, and no external sources were used.

## Finding: a tabletop harbor-history aid, beginning with a design study

**One opportunity qualifies for further investigation.** Combine the organization’s historic harbor schematic and coordinate data with its earlier raised-diagram technique and inclusive-workshop observations to develop a proposed tabletop history aid for the West Estuary Reading Room.

The useful combination is spatial: raised outlines, contrasting short labels, and spoken orientation could help a group discuss relationships among former piers and other features. This would go beyond enlarging the existing handout. **Its usefulness remains a hypothesis, and fabrication is currently blocked.**

### What is established versus hypothetical

**Established:** There is an expressed difficulty with the existing handout, a rights-cleared basis for limited prototype development, a recorded fabrication technique, and potentially relevant stock.

**Hypothesis:** A raised tabletop representation could make spatial discussion easier for some members of this particular group. Neither the earlier technique sample nor the workshop observations demonstrate that benefit. The four waypoint records alone are insufficient to design a complete tactile map.

The unexpected opportunity is the reuse of a historic display asset as a shared spatial discussion object, combining archive content with a previously unrelated making technique and access observations. No file already proposes this particular project.

### Feasibility, restrictions, and blockers

- **Design study: conditionally feasible.** A bounded internal layout and requirements exercise could fit the recorded staff allowance, subject to manager approval and access to the actual WE-98 drawing. The archive register lists it on the archive drive; the drawing itself is absent from the supplied workspace.
- **Acrylic fabrication: blocked.** November operator availability in `06-operations/laser-access.csv` does not override the failed inspection. February 2027 is an earliest stated boundary, not a confirmed return-to-service date. No alternative fabrication resource or budget is documented.
- **Materials: provisional.** Sheet count does not establish adequate dimensions, condition, texture variation, or safe assembly. The files do not specify bonding methods or a completed-object safety assessment.
- **Rights: sufficient for the stated preliminary scope.** Attribute Harbour Works and label the work as schematic historic material for non-navigation education. Broader distribution or routine public use should be checked against the terms before proceeding.
- **Other archive content is restricted.** M-41 requires separate written permission for adaptation or public display (`03-rights/survey-donation.txt`). Recorded interviews permit internal transcription only (`07-old-programmes/audio-consent.txt`). Neither is needed for this opportunity.
- **Participation and benefit: unconfirmed.** Interest in ideas is not agreement to a trial. Preferred format, suitable dimensions, handling needs, and willingness to review a sample remain unknown.
- **Capacity and funding: uncommitted.** The existing frame order and rota work remain current obligations (`08-admin/weekly-note.txt`). Historical grants and budgets do not provide present funding.

### Safe next steps and approvals

1. **Seek manager allocation for a bounded internal design study**, within the four-hour ceiling. Its deliverable should be a short concept brief and provisional layout, rather than a fabricated aid.
2. **Locate and inspect WE-98 through authorized internal access.** Compare it with the coordinate export; identify missing outlines, ambiguous relationships, and content needing verification. Do not reconstruct unsupported detail.
3. **Specify a reviewable concept:** short high-contrast labels, distinguishable tactile features, spoken orientation, Harbour Works attribution, and a prominent historic/non-navigation statement. Record unresolved material and handling questions.
4. **With organizational authorization to contact the coordinator, seek voluntary feedback on the concept.** Establish whether members want to review a sample and what would make it usable. Do not represent participation, commissioning, or accessibility benefit as agreed.
5. **Reconsider fabrication only after documented equipment clearance**, operator and manager approval, stock inspection, and an appropriate assembly/handling review. Laser use also requires extraction and a trained operator under `04-equipment/fume-guidance.md`.

**Decision:** Advance only the internal design study for approval. There is no supported basis for promising a fabricated prototype, delivery date, funded pilot, or accessibility outcome.
