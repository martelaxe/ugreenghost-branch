# Archive opportunity assessment

**Evaluation date: October 26, 2026**

**No sufficiently supported, actionable opportunity qualifies.** The archive suggests several possible combinations, but current evidence blocks delivery through resource commitments, equipment safety, reproduction rights, and absent demand. Information gathering could clarify future options; it would not establish that a pilot is feasible.

## Evidence inventory and method

I reviewed all **60 files** across correspondence, archive records, rights, equipment, learning, operations, old programmes, and administration. I used only workspace evidence and made no changes.

The principal assets are historical reference material, past teaching experience, a working camera, a repaired printer, materials, and workshop furniture. The strongest current constraints are:

- **Resources:** [`06-operations/pilot-limits.txt`](06-operations/pilot-limits.txt), dated October 19, commits all staff hours and offcuts to existing client orders until February 2027. It records no approved external funding or committed pilot partners.
- **Capacity:** [`06-operations/capacity.csv`](06-operations/capacity.csv) records zero unassigned staff hours for October 26, November 2, and November 9.
- **Safety:** [`04-equipment/laser-service.txt`](04-equipment/laser-service.txt) records a failed safety inspection on October 14 and unavailability until at least February 2027.
- **Demand:** [`01-correspondence/2026-10-reading-room.txt`](01-correspondence/2026-10-reading-room.txt) explicitly records no request or budget for new history materials and no group-session availability before March 2027.

I tested combinations against contrary evidence, ownership, dates, available resources, and prerequisites. The following are **rejected leads, not qualifying opportunities**, ordered by the strength of their underlying connection.

## 1. Accessible historical objects combining archive geometry and tactile teaching

**Hypothesis:** Historical feature coordinates, tactile construction experience, and accessibility observations might support a hands-on history object with large labels and differentiated textures.

| Evidence | What it establishes |

| [`02-archive/catalogue-1998.txt`](02-archive/catalogue-1998.txt) | Those coordinates trace third-party chart M-41; they describe former features and are not current wayfinding. |
| [`05-learning/2018-access-notes.md`](05-learning/2018-access-notes.md) | Participants informally preferred large, high-contrast labels and distinctly textured tactile lines. This was not clinical validation; no new object was requested. |
| [`05-learning/2019-tile-notes.txt`](05-learning/2019-tile-notes.txt) | A guest demonstrated stacked acrylic shapes, but owned the design; reproduction permission was not recorded. |
| [`03-rights/survey-donation.txt`](03-rights/survey-donation.txt) | M-41 and derived tracings are restricted to preservation and private reference. Copying, display, and adaptation require separate written permission. |
| [`03-rights/diagram-terms.txt`](03-rights/diagram-terms.txt) | Workshop ownership of some learning diagrams explicitly excludes M-41 tracings; eligible filenames are unspecified. |
| [`04-equipment/material-log.csv`](04-equipment/material-log.csv) | Acrylic offcuts and labeling pens are recorded, but the offcuts are held for stocktake. |
| [`04-equipment/laser-service.txt`](04-equipment/laser-service.txt) | The laser is unavailable and was not intended to produce certified safety or navigation devices. |

| [`01-correspondence/2026-10-reading-room.txt`](01-correspondence/2026-10-reading-room.txt) | The apparent potential audience has no request or budget for new materials. |

**Assessment:** This is the most distinctive combination, but its usefulness remains hypothetical. Rights, production safety, staffing, materials, and demand independently prevent a supported pilot. February is an earliest stated boundary, **not a confirmed reopening or resource allocation**.

**Safe next step and approvals:** Retain this as a deferred research question. Before development, management would need to allocate time and materials; the relevant owners would need to authorize adaptations or identifiable cleared alternatives would need to be found. Equipment safety clearance and an actual audience requirement would also be necessary. Do not reproduce the chart or guest design as an exploratory prototype without permission.
