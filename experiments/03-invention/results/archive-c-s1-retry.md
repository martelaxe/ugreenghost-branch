## 1. Tactile historical comparison object

**Provisional combination:** historical coordinates + textured construction + high-contrast labels could support an object explaining former local features.

### Supporting facts

- `02-archive/1998-waypoints.csv` supplies coordinates for four former features: stair, station, market, and hall.
- `05-learning/2018-access-notes.md` records preferences for large, high-contrast labels and tactile lines with distinct textures.
- `05-learning/2017-signage.txt` recommends testing lettering at its expected viewing distance.
- `04-equipment/material-log.csv` records 18 acrylic offcut sheets and 12 water-based paint pens.
- `05-learning/2019-tile-notes.txt` documents a historical demonstration using stacked acrylic shapes.

**Hypothesis:** these elements could form a useful historical learning object. Neither demand nor learning benefit is established.

### Contradictions, restrictions, and blockers

- `02-archive/catalogue-1998.txt` identifies the coordinates as a tracing of third-party chart M-41 and explicitly says they are **not current wayfinding**.
- `03-rights/survey-donation.txt` permits preservation and private reference only; copying, public display, and adaptation require separate written permission.
- `03-rights/diagram-terms.txt` explicitly excludes M-41 tracings from the workshop-owned diagrams.
- `05-learning/2019-tile-notes.txt` says the guest artist owned the demonstrated design; reuse permission was not recorded.
- `04-equipment/laser-service.txt`, dated October 14, 2026, says L-2 failed its safety inspection and must remain unavailable until at least February 2027.
- `06-operations/pilot-limits.txt`, dated October 19, commits all staff hours and offcuts to client orders until February 2027; no external funding or partner commitment exists.
- `01-correspondence/2026-10-reading-room.txt` states that the reading room has no request or budget for new history materials and cannot schedule another group session before March 2027.

**Assessment:** Strong evidence for the ingredients, equally strong evidence against a current pilot. February is an earliest restriction boundary, not a confirmed restart date. Accessibility observations are informal, not validation.

**Safe next step and approvals:** retain this as a deferred research question. Before commissioning work, management would need to allocate capacity and materials; the chart owner would need to authorize the intended adaptations and display; any borrowed artist design would need clearance. Production would also require a passed safety inspection and compliance with `04-equipment/fume-guidance.md`. No cutting or prototype production is currently supported.

**Provisional combination:** retained binding samples + basic print knowledge + accessibility observations could support a low-equipment, folded-paper interpretation format.

### Supporting facts

- `04-equipment/printer-repair.txt` records a July 2026 paper-feed repair.
- `01-correspondence/2026-10-reading-room.txt` says the reading room is collecting general feedback on its existing large-print handout.
- `03-rights/diagram-terms.txt` says some staff-created learning diagrams belong to the workshop.

**Hypothesis:** a newly authored paper format might eventually provide a cheaper means of testing presentation choices.

### Contradictions, restrictions, and blockers

- General feedback collection is **not a request for a replacement format, collaboration, or trial**; the same reading-room letter expressly rules out a current request and budget.
- `03-rights/diagram-terms.txt` does not identify the owned diagram filenames. No particular diagram can be treated as cleared.
- `04-equipment/printer-repair.txt` does not establish present performance across intended media; glossy media were not tested.
- `06-operations/capacity.csv` records zero unassigned hours for the weeks of October 26, November 2, and November 9.
- `06-operations/pilot-limits.txt` commits all staff hours until February 2027.
- `06-operations/stocktake.md` requires paper and damaged packaging to be checked before ordering; `08-admin/printer-paper.txt` calls for a sealed A4 reserve, not discretionary project stock.

**Assessment:** Avoiding the laser removes one dependency but does not establish demand, capacity, cleared content, or spare materials. This is a speculative development direction rather than an actionable opportunity.

**Safe next step and approvals:** if management later allocates time, inspect the retained samples and identify specific cleared content before planning a trial. Any use or adaptation of the reading room’s handout would require agreement covering the proposed use. A partner’s willingness to participate remains unconfirmed.

**Hypothesis:** combining spoken accounts and images might support a future indoor history session. Historical attendance and borrowing do not demonstrate a current audience or partner.

### Contradictions, restrictions, and blockers

- `02-archive/chart-register.csv` marks P-12 portrait releases as unknown.
- `03-rights/photograph-checklist.md` requires verification of authorship and subject permission; unknown permissions are not clearance.
- `07-old-programmes/2022-walk.txt` says the route and organizers have not been reconfirmed.

**Assessment:** Multiple historical assets exist, but rights, partners, staffing, funding, and demand are unresolved. Neither playback nor public image reuse is currently supported.

**Safe next step and approvals:** any future management-authorized scoping should first establish which recordings and images could receive appropriate permissions. New recording consent and verified image rights would precede public use. No outreach, event booking, or publication is justified by the archive alone.
