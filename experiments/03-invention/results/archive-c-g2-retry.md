# Archive opportunity assessment

**Evaluation date: October 26, 2026**

**No sufficiently supported, actionable opportunity was identified.** The archive supports an interesting combination for accessible history learning, but its essential rights, equipment, materials, staff capacity, and partner demand are blocked or unconfirmed. Presenting it as a viable pilot would overstate the evidence.

I examined the files in the workspace without modifying them or using external sources. Paths below are relative to the workspace root.

## Closest candidate: accessible history interpretation through paper and tactile materials

The potential combination is a history teaching object pairing large, high-contrast text with differentiated tactile features, possibly using folded paper for initial exploration. This joins archived historical geometry, retained accessibility observations, physical teaching samples, and fabrication experience.

That is an investigator-generated hypothesis, **not an existing approved project or a qualifying opportunity**.

### Facts versus hypotheses

**Established:** historical spatial data, informal accessibility observations, and past fabrication and paper-learning techniques exist. The records also establish substantive restrictions and current resource commitments.

**Hypothetical:** combining these ingredients could produce a useful learning object. The archive does not establish that anyone wants it, that paper would deliver effective tactile differentiation, that the historical content is sufficiently accurate for interpretation, or that the result would improve accessibility.

The reading room’s invitation for general handout feedback does **not** establish demand for a new object or consent to participate in a pilot.

### Feasibility, restrictions, and blockers

- **Rights:** neither the coordinate export nor the guest artist’s design is cleared for adaptation. Workshop ownership of unspecified diagrams is insufficient to select an alternative.
- **Equipment and safety:** the laser is unavailable. February 2027 is an earliest stated boundary, not a promised return to service. `04-equipment/fume-guidance.md` additionally requires extraction, a trained operator, and a current service check.
- **Capacity and materials:** `06-operations/capacity.csv` records zero unassigned staff hours for the weeks beginning October 26, November 2, and November 9. Existing stock cannot be treated as spare project material.
- **Demand and funding:** no customer, participating group, approved budget, or committed partner is established.
- **Validation:** the retained observations do not support accessibility certification or claims of demonstrated benefit. Historical geometry must not become operational navigation guidance.

### Safe next steps and needed approvals

**Now:** retain this as a deferred research question; do not fabricate, reproduce restricted content, purchase materials, or approach partners on the assumption that a pilot exists.

**If management later chooses to explore it:** first approve a bounded staff allocation and identify an actual learning need. Then identify specific rights-cleared content or obtain the required written permissions. Any later prototype would also need released materials, an approved budget where necessary, and suitable equipment and safety clearance. Participant involvement would require an agreed purpose and appropriate permission.

A paper approach might avoid dependence on the laser, but it would not resolve content rights, staff capacity, demand, or evaluation needs.

## Other observations—not qualifying opportunities

- **Audio interpretation is restricted.** `07-old-programmes/sound-log.csv` lists recordings limited to internal transcription, and `07-old-programmes/audio-consent.txt` excludes public playback or reuse without new consent. Their existence does not establish a usable public audio resource.
- **Past activity does not establish present backing.** `07-old-programmes/2019-grant-notice.txt` concerns a closed 2019 funding cycle; `07-old-programmes/old-grant-budget.txt` is historical expenditure. `07-old-programmes/2022-walk.txt` says the route and organizers have not been reconfirmed.
- **The chair inquiry is an unresolved operational lead.** `01-correspondence/2026-september.txt` records an inquiry, and `03-rights/room-policy.txt` permits room renters to use furniture. Those facts do not establish permission for off-site chair hire, available inventory, or a novel combination supported by five independent files.

The archive therefore supports a **deferred possibility with explicit prerequisites**, rather than an actionable opportunity as of the evaluation date.
