# Research plan — Experiment 03

## Prior evidence and limits

Experiment 01 used 12 short invented files; a fresh Codex session found a planted invoice discrepancy. Experiment 02 used two 22-file variants and found an explicit exhibition call matching a photo archive; both reports followed the permission change. Those tests lacked a generic baseline, repeated runs, a true negative, realistic scale and NAS execution. Their read-only sandboxes did not prevent reads outside the workspace, though logged investigator commands remained within it. Local reports were not evidence of UGREEN performance.

The official `uliya-skill-kit` exposes a catalog of delegated experts and a static checker, not a NAS. The prior capability audit establishes only documented actions, not that they work end-to-end on the device. Read-only sandboxing and web search disabling do not isolate the model provider or all local reads.

## Question and hypotheses

Can an investigator construct a useful, testable new combination across a fictional archive; recognize a decisive counterfactual blocker; and avoid promoting unsupported combinations? Does an explicit domain-independent investigation method improve results over a generic prompt? The null possibility is equal or worse performance for the specialized prompt. The task is a synthetic approximation of creative recombination, not proof of human novelty or general invention.

## Design

Construct archive A with 50–70 realistically accumulated, varied records and an evidence-supported recombination involving five independent facts, without an explicit preexisting proposal. Archive B differs by exactly one decisive fact. Archive C is a comparably sized, separately fictional negative control with plausible but unconfirmed or blocked combinations. A strict qualifying opportunity requires a specific materially new combination, credible first action, evidence from at least five independent source files, and no decisive documented blocker; cautious investigative follow-up is not the same as an already actionable pilot. Preserve multiple valid alternative findings when independently supported.

Freeze the answer key, negative-control rationale, 30-point rubric and both investigator prompts before running. Execute 2 fresh independent Codex sessions for each archive × prompt cell (12 total), using the same CLI options, model configuration and access; G and S share the same objective and deliverables, but S receives a longer procedure, so prompt length and explicit scaffolding are bundled rather than separately identified. No silent retries for weak reasoning; retry only documented technical failure. Collect and save all reports before scoring.

Analyze per-run scores, genuine opportunity identification, evidence coverage, barrier awareness, false positives, negative-control abstention, alternatives and repetition variation. Report raw numbers and uncertainty without significance claims. Comparison cannot isolate a single component of the longer prompt or generalize from one positive and one negative archive.

Maintain complete hashes, diffs, process events, exact prompts, token usage when available, model configuration, exit status and observed limitations. Workspace evidence is copied outside the repository, with no symlinks or guide/key files; runtime may still read outside via its standard read-only sandbox. Keep private answers out of investigator workspaces and do not consciously supply the answer in the prompts. Do not assert NAS compatibility until documented capabilities are separately audited.
