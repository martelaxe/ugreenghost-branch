# Comparative evaluation — Experiment 03

**Evaluated after all 12 fresh retry reports were saved**, against the preregistered 30-point rubric. Author and evaluator are the same person; scores are subjective, not independent expert ratings. First attempts are excluded as technical failures, not weak substantive answers; their outputs are preserved. Full report paths below are relative to `experiments/03-invention`.

## Results

All 12 valid reports scored **30/30** under the preregistered rubric and passed its gate. The coarse rubric and deliberately legible clues created a ceiling effect; this is not a claim of perfect reliability or equality outside this benchmark.

| Workflow | Runs | A: intended combination | B: blocker recognized | C: abstained from ready pilot | False-positive pilots | Mean score |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Generic G | 6 | 2/2 | 2/2 | 2/2 | 0/6 | 30/30 |
| Specialized S | 6 | 2/2 | 2/2 | 2/2 | 0/6 | 30/30 |

All four A reports independently propose a bounded raised historical discussion aid from existing assets. All four B reports identify the same combination and L-2's prohibition; no B report recommends operating it before clearance. All four C reports explicitly say no qualifying near-term pilot exists. Zero reports promoted the defined restricted M-41, audio or expired grant as usable or recommended unsupported operation of L-2. “0/6” counts explicit unsupported **qualifying pilots** under the rubric, not every hypothetical lead; absence of a claim is not proof of a successful adversarial test.

No distinct alternative pilot was established by five independently relevant files without a critical blocker. S's negative-control reports explore paper/print, recorded audio, or historical walks as **rejected hypotheses**, not new demonstrated opportunities. C's general feedback and chair inquiry are real opportunities to inquire, but do not satisfy the preregistered five-source novel-pilot standard; the archive does not prove every conceivable alternative is impossible. C is synthetic and deliberately constrained; treat its negative label as a design assertion, not an independently verified truth.

### Evidence supporting each dimension

- **A identification and recombination (6 + 6 per run):** each A report connects the expressed group need in `01-correspondence/2026-10-reading-room.txt` with old staff-created content in `02-archive/catalogue-1998.txt`/`02-archive/1998-waypoints.csv`, permission in `03-rights/diagram-terms.txt`, the technique in `05-learning/2019-tile-notes.txt`, equipment in `04-equipment/laser-service.txt`, and conditional staff allocation in `06-operations/pilot-limits.txt`. For example, A-G1 calls it “a small raised tabletop harbor-history prototype” and A-S2 calls it a “small internal prototype of a raised, high-contrast tabletop harbor-history diagram.” All four propose the combination, not an already-existing proposal.
- **B identification and recombination (6 + 6 per run):** B-G1 and B-G2 call it a design/feasibility lead, not a currently manufacturable pilot; B-S1 and B-S2 explicitly classify it as a rejected blocked candidate while preserving the same multi-file connection. Under the frozen B rubric, identifying a blocked combination earns identification credit. All four cite the failed `04-equipment/laser-service.txt` and distinguish conditional operator slots from actual clearance.
- **C identification and recombination (6 + 6 per run):** C-G1, C-G2, C-S1 and C-S2 explicitly abstain from a qualifying pilot, and each ties together at least five relevant sources for a blocked hypothetical: third-party tracing `02-archive/catalogue-1998.txt`, restricted `03-rights/survey-donation.txt`, failed `04-equipment/laser-service.txt`, committed `06-operations/pilot-limits.txt`, reading-room `01-correspondence/2026-10-reading-room.txt` and access notes `05-learning/2018-access-notes.md`.
- **Traceability (5 per run):** each report gives concrete path-level support for material claims, including the need, asset, rights and key feasibility constraints. All reports were inspected; no material invented customer, resource or guaranteed payment was found. Some descriptions summarize rather than quote the source, so this does not prove every incidental sentence is error-free.
- **Actionability (5 per run):** A reports request manager approval, verify equipment/stock and limit the prototype; B reports halt laser fabrication and propose bounded design/review with approval. C reports defer production and explain what authorization or information would be necessary. B-G2's paper-layout suggestion is explicitly conditional on approval and available paper, and acknowledges it would not validate a tactile aid.
- **Uncertainty/blockers (4 per run):** A distinguishes historic representation from navigation and informal observations from validated benefit; B identifies the failed inspection and does not equate February with clearance; C identifies the failed laser and restricted chart plus absent capacity and/or current demand.
- **False-positive control (4 per run):** no report recommends adapting M-41, publicly replaying restricted audio, relying on the expired grant, or using historical data as current navigation. Several explicitly reject these leads. No unsupported financial benefit was asserted.

### Pairwise and descriptive observations

- A discovery: G **2/2**, S **2/2**; B safe blocker recognition: G **2/2**, S **2/2**; C abstention: G **2/2**, S **2/2**. Each repeated run reached the same category-level disposition.
- B framing differs: G describes a worthwhile **feasibility/design step**, while S says no currently actionable **fabrication opportunity** qualifies. Both withhold laser use and suggest bounded review; this looks like a stricter label or threshold, not proven better safety.
- S's reports considered more rejected candidate families explicitly; the two C-S reports describe three each. This is a qualitative description of report content, not validated extra discovery or measured benefit.
- Mean observed duration: G **55.77 s**, S **69.80 s**. Mean total input tokens: G **55,209**, S **63,470**; mean output tokens: G **1,704**, S **2,226**. Input counts include cached input tokens; they are not the number billed or a cost estimate. Concurrency, service load and unequal prompt lengths confound timing. Per-run usage and duration are retained in `execution/retry-status.json`.
- **Conclusion:** S did not outperform G on the preregistered metrics in this sample. The prompts differ in length and procedural instructions together; we cannot infer the effect of any one component. No statistical significance or generalization claim is justified.

### Validity limitations

The designer/evaluator knew the intended answer; there was no independent blinded human scoring. The 60-file archives are still short, fictional, single-language text with one salient domain, and C intentionally shares the same broad schema as A/B. Some ordinary records are near duplicates. A nearby filename, explicit need, employee-owned map, and explicit techniques make the recombination relatively legible. Two replicates per cell are too few for error rates; a capped rubric causes ceiling effects. The read-only sandbox is not a complete read-isolation boundary, and although project access was mode-restricted during execution, workspaces shared a temporary parent and Codex authentication/model backend. Logs show only workspace-relative evidence reads, not proof of all internal process behavior. No real NAS or UGREEN runtime participated.
