# UGREEN evidence contract audit

**Conclusion:** the installed kit documents useful actions and a generic success/error envelope, but it does **not** define sufficiently precise real expert-return contracts to establish reliable provenance, complete coverage, truncation detection or cross-agent transfer for the proposed discovery workflow. The audit is of toolkit 0.7.3 and catalog 2.0.0, not real NAS execution.

## Sources and general boundary

The installed catalog is [capability-catalog.json](/home/martelaxe/ugreen-hackathon/resources/uliya-skill-kit/data/capability-catalog.json:1), SHA-256 `c927fd8e233696cd152baf98468368912c13c08aabeac353f0ee95c46c0428e6`, matching the installed kit copy. The [`resultEnvelope`](/home/martelaxe/ugreen-hackathon/resources/uliya-skill-kit/data/capability-catalog.json:6) documents illustrative generic CLI success `{ok: true, data: {}}` and failure `{ok: false, error: {}}`; it does not give a per-action schema or a delegate-result envelope. All seven audited actions mark permissions, prerequisites, error codes, example output status and stability as `unknown`.

The [development standard](/home/martelaxe/ugreen-hackathon/resources/uliya-skill-kit/docs/skill-development-standard.md:1) identifies registered experts as executors; [expert orchestration](/home/martelaxe/ugreen-hackathon/resources/uliya-skill-kit/references/expert-orchestration.md:1) instructs passing verified business results between them. [Completion and errors](/home/martelaxe/ugreen-hackathon/resources/uliya-skill-kit/references/completion-and-errors.md:1) specifies handling `completed`, `failed`, `cancelled`, `waiting_user`, and `waiting_approval`, but does not define their serialized envelope or complete error taxonomy. [Safety and approval](/home/martelaxe/ugreen-hackathon/resources/uliya-skill-kit/references/safety-and-approval.md:1) defers actual validation and approval to the child workflow. The parent must delegate rather than directly execute CLI actions ([usage rules](/home/martelaxe/ugreen-hackathon/resources/uliya-skill-kit/references/cli-usage-rules.md:1)).

## Experiment 04 assumptions and actual implications

Experiment 04 invented a wrapper `{status, result}` around the generic envelope and fixture fields `paths`, `documents`, `text`, `complete`, `coverage.complete`, `evidence` records with `role`, `path`, `quote`, and `blocker`. See its [simulation](/home/martelaxe/ugreen-hackathon/experiments/04-ugreen-orchestration/simulation/mock_workflow.py:1). The catalog's prose occasionally mentions an `evidence` or `coverage` concept, but **not** the mock's precise nesting, requiredness or semantics. The mock tests handling of its own assumptions, not UGREEN contracts.

The kit tells the parent to transfer real verified results and stop on failed/pending states, yet does not prove that every relevant source identifier, excerpt and coverage signal exists or survives the handoff. The proposed parent-generated report is a design inference, not a documented final-response API. A successful call, static Skill check, `full` option or quoted summary cannot establish complete archive coverage.

**NAS validation needed:** collect sanitized real success, empty, denied, unsupported-format, truncated/limited, failed and pending results for each stage; establish actual delegation state nesting, source identity/quoted location, coverage/continuation, and lossless handoff. Until then, missing provenance or completeness must be reported as unknown and no finding should be represented as comprehensively verified. The catalog-level gaps are material to a trustworthy “Surprise Me” feature, although they do not prove the runtime cannot supply these details.
