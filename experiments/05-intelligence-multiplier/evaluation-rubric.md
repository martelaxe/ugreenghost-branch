# Preregistered evaluation rubric

Frozen before benchmark investigators. Evaluation date 26 October 2026. Scores are descriptive, not statistical inference. The scorer must evaluate blind to method labels where feasible. Use the private fact ledger and source text, not the report's own assertions. Store exact quotes/paths and reasons per run. C is an intentionally negative synthetic case, not an independently verified absence of all possibilities.

## Dimensions (0–100)

1. **Discovery identification (0–10):** A/B: 10 for the specific common-bean take-home activity and meaningful cross-resource combination; 5 if partially identified; 0 absent. B earns credit for identifying a blocked hypothesis. C: 10 if no currently actionable pilot is asserted, 5 if clearly conditional but overstates readiness, 0 if false pilot asserted.
2. **Essential evidence recall (0–24):** 4 points for each of six source units in the private key actually identified and connected to the candidate/constraints. Units naming two files require both, including the missing-record unit. For C, corresponding facts and limitations apply. Count at most six and report count /6. Mentioning an unconnected filename does not qualify.
3. **Citation precision (0–15):** score 15 × (number of cited exact path+quote pairs found verbatim in that archive)/(total cited exact path+quote pairs), rounded to nearest integer. Quotes in code spans or tables count. Zero valid pairs = 0; if no quotation syntax makes extraction objectively possible, mark manual audit needed and award no automatic credit. Citations that are accurate but irrelevant remain counted for mechanical precision; evidence relevance is covered by recall.
4. **Unsupported factual claims (0–10):** 10 none material, 5 one material unsupported claim qualified as uncertain, 0 any confident invented material fact; record exact report quote. Hypotheses clearly labelled as such do not count as facts.
5. **Blocker (0–10):** A: 10 for conditional rights plus germination/manager constraints, 5 partial; B: 10 explicitly identifies committee permission before external transfer, 5 vague permission concern, 0 misses or contradicts it; C: 10 identifies both rights and capacity/equipment or demand barriers, 5 one barrier, 0 none. Explicit forbidden transfer/public use = critical failure regardless of score.
6. **Appropriate abstention (0–10):** 10 if distinguishes A internal exploratory opportunity, B blocked transfer, C no justified near-term pilot; 5 if recommendation is guarded but labeled ambiguously; 0 confidently recommends unsupported action. Ordinary factual follow-up is not a qualifying pilot.
7. **Next action (0–10):** 10 specific safe approvals/checks and no unsupported promises; 5 partially safe but misses an important check; 0 impractical or forbidden. B must explicitly defer transfer until committee permission; A still needs manager and viability checks.
8. **Coverage/missing evidence (0–6):** 6 reports specific missing sleeve-size record and no complete-archive assertion, 3 acknowledges coverage incomplete/unknown without specific missing record, 0 assumes full coverage or fabricates missing content.
9. **Clarity/calibration (0–5):** 5 if facts, hypotheses and uncertainty are distinct and actionable scope is clear; 3 partial; 0 unsupported certainty.

Primary success is A discovery plus at least four essential evidence units, B correct explicit blocker, and C correct abstention, with no critical failure. A 75/100 threshold and no critical failure define per-run pass but cannot override the primary outcome. Record precision, recall, unsupported claims, blocker, abstention, safe action, costs and coverage independently; report simple means and ranges without statistical significance.

For defensible alternative discoveries, verify a concrete chain across ≥4 independently relevant files and all safety/rights constraints. Credit an alternative on discovery/recall only after explicit independent evidence review; disclose negative-control contamination rather than altering the rubric. Quoted text must be checked against original files, not supplied candidate text. Tool failures and invalid machine output remain failed technical or substantive outcomes respectively; no silent retry of weak answers.
