# Decision memo — Experiment 05

**Decision: keep “Surprise Me” as a narrow, on-demand hypothesis with visible sources and uncertainty; do not claim an intelligence multiplier or a verified NAS implementation.** The evidence contract and real runtime need validation before we expand the product.

1. **UGREEN contracts:** The kit documents agent/action inputs, a generic success/error envelope and required handling of delegate states. Its output summaries do not define guaranteed source paths, excerpts, coverage, truncation, pagination or cross-agent handoff fields. Those gaps prevent a promise of comprehensive verification.
2. **Weak model and valid runs:** 18/18 investigations completed technically, each explicitly configured as `gpt-6-luna` with `low` effort. The CLI event stream did not independently echo the backend model identity. A separate scorer was requested as `gpt-6.1-sol`/`high`; it was not a benchmark method.
3. **G vs P vs H:** Mean scores were **87.00**, **84.33** and **44.83** out of 100 respectively. Generic and specialized prompt trials identified the positive case twice and recognized the B blocker twice. H identified the positive once and the B blocker once; two of its verifier calls returned no finding despite a relevant candidate.
4. **Cost of improvement:** No H improvement was demonstrated. H used **two model calls per trial**, versus one for G/P. Mean total input tokens were 35,965 for H versus 54,112 G and 51,290 P; cached portions differed substantially. CLI counters are not the user's billing records, and concurrent elapsed times do not establish cost savings.
5. **Ceiling and remaining difficulty:** The differentiated 100-point rubric produced varied scores, so the prior uniform-score ceiling did not recur. The archives remain small, synthetic and text-heavy. Source omission, false abstention and inaccurate file-count reporting remain difficult; the scorer was blinded by case labels but report style could reveal method.
6. **Distinctive feature:** A “surprise” should show a specific cross-file hypothesis, exact supporting evidence, an important blocker, coverage limitations and one safe next step. A generic model already produced that kind of result in these tests; the product must prove value through trustworthy NAS integration and experience, not the current harness.
7. **Next engineering task:** Obtain actual UGREEN delegate outputs or organizer clarification for source identifiers, excerpts, partial coverage, truncation, states and handoff; then run one small read-only counterfactual workflow on the real NAS when access is available. If evidence or coverage cannot be established, explicitly degrade or abstain.
