**Confirmed:** The indexed subjects and dates fit the request, and the agreement potentially covers the required uses.

**Hypothesis:** B-17 contains at least 12–20 suitable, documented, cleared images. Only five are individually indexed in the supplied files.

### Feasibility and obstacles

The [capacity schedule](04-production/studio-capacity.csv) lists 23 archive hours across the four weeks beginning October 26, though later availability depends on bookings. This supports preliminary investigation before the deadline.

- Negative condition, captions, final production requirements, and total costs remain unknown.
- The [large-format printer](04-production/printer-service.txt) awaits a part expected in December. External printing has no recorded quote.
- The maximum fee is neither guaranteed revenue nor demonstrated profit.

2. Record image-specific permissions questions and reconcile the job-note chronology before representing any image as cleared.

4. Submit by November 20 **only through the centre’s official process**, if the checks support submission.
