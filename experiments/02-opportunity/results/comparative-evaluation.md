## Reasons and citations

### 1. Discovery accuracy

- **A — 2/2.** `results/archive-a-report.md`, finding 1, connects the active centre request in `01-inbox/2026-10-centre-note.txt` to the historic B-17 collection via `02-archive/harbor-index.csv`, `02-archive/box-inventory.csv` and `02-archive/legacy-catalogue-note.md`. It calls the match an opportunity rather than a commission.
- **B — 2/2.** `results/archive-b-report.md`, finding 1, identifies the same match and says “No exhibition income or new booking is established.”

### 2. Cross-file evidence

- **A — 2/2.** The report names the call, index, inventory, catalogue note, Vale agreement, 1991 job note, scanner, workflow estimate and capacity schedule. It uses them to explain thematic fit, creator rights, employment provenance and logistics.
- **B — 2/2.** The report connects the same evidential categories and cites `03-rights/vale-archive-agreement.txt`, `06-projects/1991-quay-job-note.txt`, `04-production/scanner-inventory.txt`, `04-production/scan-estimate.txt` and `04-production/studio-capacity.csv`, in addition to the call and archive index. Both exceed the four-source threshold.

### 3. Usefulness

- **A — 2/2.** Finding 1 recommends inspecting B-17, checking image condition, authorship, captions and permissions, costing the proposal, and using the centre’s official process before 20 November if checks support it.
- **B — 2/2.** Finding 1 recommends inspecting candidates, seeking Elena’s written permission “including submission samples where applicable,” checking individual rights, budgeting, and submitting only if requirements can be met. It does not advocate unauthorized sharing.

### 4. Uncertainty

- **A — 2/2.** It says only five images are individually indexed, classifies the presence of 12–20 suitable images as a hypothesis, treats the fee as conditional, and flags privacy, chronology, printing and cost uncertainty.
- **B — 2/2.** It says 12–20 suitable images have not been established; creator permission and individual rights remain unresolved; selection and payment are not guaranteed. It also recognizes timing and print-cost uncertainties.

### 5. False-positive control

- **A — 2/2.** “Leads excluded” explicitly cites `03-rights/donor-letter.txt` and rejects public use of B-27; it also cites `01-inbox/old-festival-call.txt` and notes its April 2025 deadline. The wedding inquiry is presented as an unresolved lead, not guaranteed revenue.
- **B — 2/2.** The report explicitly excludes B-27 based on the donor letter and notes that the festival call expired in April 2025. Its additional wedding and Cedar invoice leads include relevant uncertainties; it does not claim that the invoice is overdue or that the weekly reminder refers to Cedar.

### 6. Counterfactual rights

- **A — 2/2.** It explicitly names the non-exclusive, attribution-dependent creator license and qualifies that it does not settle individual releases or every image’s coverage. Source: `03-rights/vale-archive-agreement.txt`; report section “Evidence and connection” and “Feasibility and obstacles.”
- **B — 2/2.** It states that public use is “expressly withheld without separate written approval” and recommends obtaining written permission, including for samples where applicable. It also notes privacy and other rights are separate questions. Source: the same agreement path in the B variant.

## Gate and interpretation

Both reports explain the opportunity, cite at least four relevant independent evidence files, identify blockers and propose practical steps. Both exceed 9/12, score at least 1 in each category and score 2 on rights. The pair also meets the preregistered comparative criterion: A describes a conditional existing creator license; B requires separate written permission before the proposed use.

These are two individual local Codex runs on deliberately constructed text records. They demonstrate that the investigators found the planted relationship and responded appropriately to the altered clause in this pair; they do not establish reliability on real archives, prove the difference was caused solely by the clause across repeated runs, or demonstrate compatibility with the UGREEN NAS or its permitted tools. The opportunity is partly discoverable through an explicit call and a structured index; the experiment tests cross-file synthesis, not unassisted identification of an entirely unknown market.
