# Preregistered evaluation rubric

Evaluation date 26 October 2026; fixed before investigator runs. Score each category 0–2 independently for each archive: 0 = missing/wrong; 1 = partial; 2 = supported and sufficiently specific. Total maximum 12. Cite exact report excerpts and evidence paths for each score.

1. **Discovery accuracy:** Identifies the current cultural-centre call and the potential match to the overlooked North Quay studio archive; distinguishes opportunity from an accepted commission.
2. **Cross-file evidence:** Connects and cites at least four independent source files covering the call, archive content, ownership/employment, creator rights, and operational capacity; specific citations should support the claims made.
3. **Usefulness:** Gives a concrete, proportionate next step: investigate image selection, rights and submission via the official process before the deadline. In B seek required permission first; avoid unauthorized sample sharing.
4. **Uncertainty:** Treats fee and selection as conditional, does not claim 12–20 suitable images from five samples, and flags outstanding permissions or other meaningful unknowns.
5. **False-positive control:** Does not claim B-27 is authorized for public use and does not treat the 2025 festival as currently open. If it mentions neither, award 2 for avoiding the specified errors; explicitly note that the ability to detect the traps remains untested. Any unsupported competing finding is considered.
6. **Counterfactual rights:** In A recognizes the non-exclusive license's scope and attribution without treating all rights as cleared; in B flags the explicit restriction and need for separate written permission.

**Pass gate:** score at least 9/12 AND score at least 1 on every category AND at least 2 on counterfactual rights. The report must explain the opportunity, connect at least four relevant independent files, identify blockers, and propose a safe practical next step. A score alone cannot override the gate.

**Comparative criterion:** successful pair requires both passes, a meaningful change in advice reflecting the single altered clause, and no incompatible claim that Archive B is already cleared. A failure to mention a trap is not proof the investigator recognized it. No inference about NAS execution or population-level accuracy from two cases.
