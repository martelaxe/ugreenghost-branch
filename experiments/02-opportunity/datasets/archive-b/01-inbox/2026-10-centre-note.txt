From: North Quay Cultural Centre, collections desk
Date: 8 October 2026
Subject: Request for proposals — local working waterfront

We are preparing a community exhibition about the working waterfront, 1980–1995. We welcome proposals of 12–20 historically documented photographs from local contributors. The exhibition and its online preview are planned for March 2027; public display and non-exclusive online publicity permissions are required. A contributor fee of up to $1,200 is available, subject to selection, contracting and funding.

Please send a short description, sample contact sheets and a note on rights and captions by 20 November 2026. Unsolicited material should be sent only through the centre's official submission process.
