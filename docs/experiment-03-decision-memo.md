# Experiment 03 decision memo

1. **Did AI invent a useful, unexpected opportunity?** In one designed synthetic setting, 4/4 A investigators proposed a plausible new combination: a small tactile historic-harbor discussion aid from an old staff-owned schematic, existing workshop technique/materials and an expressed access difficulty. It is a hypothesis for a manager-approved internal sample, not proven useful to participants or novel to humans.
2. **Did it recognize the critical obstacle?** All 4/4 B investigators identified the failed laser inspection and deferred fabrication. They differed in whether safe concept review counted as an “opportunity.”
3. **Did it avoid unsupported opportunities?** All 4/4 C investigators declined to recommend a ready pilot; no report promoted the preregistered unsafe/restricted traps as cleared. C is synthetic and its negative label was not independently reviewed.
4. **Did specialized beat generic?** No measurable advantage: G and S both averaged 30/30 in their six runs under a coarse rubric, with a substantial ceiling effect. The longer S prompt was slower on average in these concurrent runs, but latency is confounded and the sample is tiny.
5. **What UGREEN operations are documented?** The official catalog lists scoped file discovery and reading, document metadata/extraction/comparison, evidence collection and approval-gated text saving/moves. Creative multi-source reasoning and reliable citations are not proven on NAS. See [UGREEN feasibility](../experiments/03-invention/ugreen-feasibility.md).
6. **Biggest technical risk:** supported expert agents may fail to extract enough of a mixed or large archive, preserve source references and surface decisive constraints within the real model and execution limits. The successful Codex runs read all 60 small text files directly; no NAS execution occurred.
7. **Single distinguishing capability:** an on-demand **evidence-grounded surprise** that proposes one useful cross-file possibility, shows its supporting sources and explicit blockers, and separates a safe investigation from an approved action.
8. **What next?** Validate one narrow end-to-end UGREEN orchestration on permitted representative files before expanding the concept. The workflow should read and cite evidence, surface a counterfactual blocker and report incomplete coverage.

## Recommended engineering tasks

1. Map a minimal on-demand workflow to the installed catalog, including exact agents, input scope, bounded retrieval, evidence handoff and failure/approval states; do not yet package a submission.
2. Build a small local mock of expert return envelopes and test missing files, truncated evidence, conflicting dates and blocked actions. Label it a simulation.
3. Create fresh held-out archives with a different author and an independent scorer; include mixed document types and a negative control before rerunning G vs an S2 prompt.
4. Ask the organizer or test the real NAS when available for actual format support, cross-file provenance, context limits, approval states and local-model behavior. Avoid claims of background monitoring, a graph UI or time-travel restore until documented and verified.
