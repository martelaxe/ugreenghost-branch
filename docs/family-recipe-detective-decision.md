# Family Recipe Detective — niche prototype decision

**Concept:** recover one family's recipe from scattered source materials, while keeping competing family variants and missing instructions visible. The user can ask a short question; the planned outcome is a source-backed draft, an optional safely saved text record, and optionally an approved album of related photos if real UGREEN handoffs support it.

**Why this direction:** It follows a concrete pattern found in popular marketplace Skills: a specific recurring problem, multi-step workflow, source-backed decisions and an end result. It also has more expressive potential than Before You Move: the user sees family memories, a practical recipe and an unresolved mystery, not only a pre-change warning. [Marketplace review and source links](../research/family-recipe-detective/market-and-boundaries.md). This is a design judgment; it has not been rated by actual NAS users or judges.

**What exists:** [Markdown-only Skill](../prototype/family-recipe-detective/SKILL.md), with directly linked reconstruction, expert-handoff and output references. It delegates file, document, photo and knowledge tasks to documented registered experts; it handles missing and conflicting evidence, incomplete coverage, permissions, pending approvals and optional actions. The official checker passed with no errors or warnings. [Static result](../experiments/family-recipe-detective/static-check.json).

**What was observed:** two independent local Codex runs read the same fictional image and notes. Both recognized a milk-versus-water difference and missing cooking details; the Skill added a source ledger and explicitly qualified the missing recording. An [illustrative local image](../experiments/family-recipe-detective/illustrative-recipe-preview.png) depicts fixture facts but is not the UGREEN interface or Skill rendering. [Actual results](../experiments/family-recipe-detective/test-results.md).

**What is not established:** the generic assistant achieved the central finding too. No human found the product useful or surprising. [Touta](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/touta-family-recipe-keeper/id6755372509) already advertises photo, voice and audio recipe capture, and [Recipes We Share](https://recipesweshare.com/app) advertises handwritten-card scanning and shared cookbooks. This direction **substantially overlaps existing products**. Its NAS-specific, conflict-aware workflow might be convenient; it is not established as original or better.

Actual audio transcription, provenance propagation, user-selected photo IDs, `add-item` encoding, album creation/membership, write collision behavior, approvals, local-versus-cloud processing and NAS execution are unverified. The in-chat draft is the primary outcome. Optional save and album are conditional and must not be claimed complete without real expert results. Do not assume safe cooking, allergy suitability, or original family authorship from the AI's inference.

**Decision:** a richer, concrete niche **prototype to review**, not a proven winning concept or final submission. Before choosing this over the earlier candidate, inspect the actual example, then test real expert outputs during the finalist session if selected and seek authentic user feedback. No final PDF, Skill ZIP, upload, publication, commit or submission was created in this turn.
