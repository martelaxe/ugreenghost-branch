# Experiment 09 — Creative Salvage decision

**Decision: REDESIGN; no demonstrated competitive advantage yet.** Creative Salvage produced plausible, source-grounded ideas and in this small sample its structured prompt avoided a risky fallback seen in one generic response. But generic Codex also produced the principal creative combination and the useful alternative. One specialized response had broken citations and an incorrect inventory count. No real creator or UGREEN NAS participated. Preserve this as a testable candidate, with **Knowledge Succession Rehearsal** as the fallback; do not claim a distinct intelligence mechanism or finalize the submission around it yet.

## Competitor overlap

[Adobe Experience Manager Assets](https://business.adobe.com/products/experience-manager/assets/digital-rights-management.html) advertises asset reuse and rights management. [Contesimal](https://contesimal.ai/) advertises turning historical content libraries into new opportunities. [Weaviate Foundry](https://weaviate.io/blog/building-foundry-creative-search) describes read-only creative-archive search with links back to source files. [SOMEDAY](https://devpost.com/software/someday-vzdi0f) describes giving abandoned creative projects a second life via AI exhibit cards and collaboration. These sources establish substantial pieces of overlap; they are product/project descriptions, not hands-on product validation or proof of identical full workflows. A narrow, evidence-backed creative decision might still differentiate in practice, but this experiment has not demonstrated it. Details and the falsifiable thesis are in [competitive thesis](../experiments/09-creative-salvage/competitive-thesis.md).

## Strongest actual proposal

One fictional archive joins an abandoned changing-shoreline workshop with a separate collection of written water-level observations and captions. The strongest output is a **small participatory paper-and-text display**, subject to checking current materials, venue, printing and the specific usage terms. Generic trial [A-G-2](../experiments/09-creative-salvage/execution/A-G-2.md) and specialized trial [A-S-1](../experiments/09-creative-salvage/execution/A-S-1.md) both proposed versions of it. This is a real model output based on fictional text, not a generated physical exhibit; there are no actual images or recordings in the archive. The fixture authors designed the thematic connection, so this does not establish that an independent creator would find it surprising.

When the second project's permission changes, [B-S-1](../experiments/09-creative-salvage/execution/B-S-1.md) correctly separates a blocked pattern from a narrower display using the first project's explicitly permitted text. Both generic trials also discovered a text-only alternative. Generic [B-G-1](../experiments/09-creative-salvage/execution/B-G-1.md) suggested a fallback from the movable-band concept without reproducing the restricted pattern; a blinded evaluator flagged the risk that this could still be an unsupported adaptation. That is a potential safety weakness, not a legal determination.

## Comparative evidence and safety

Twelve independent fresh Codex investigator processes completed: two generic and two specialized runs for each of three 31-file fictional variants, with the same requested model and task. Three fresh AI evaluators scored anonymized outputs without the method mapping. Their unmodified totals were **67/72 generic** and **71/72 specialized**. The rubric was ceiling-heavy and one AI evaluator scored each case; a four-point aggregate difference is not a reliable, population-level advantage. Specialized B handled the specific restriction better in these two trials, but alternative discovery itself was not unique.

A post-review mechanical check found **four malformed Markdown citation targets** in specialized C-S-2 when checked against its original investigator workspace; generic outputs had none there. All 12 raw reports also used the temporary `/tmp/work/` mount in their links, which does not resolve on the host after the process exits. Normalizing that prefix for review makes underlying files identifiable but is not a result delivered by either method. That same report said it reviewed 27 files when the captured inventory and file reads showed 31. Generic C-G-1 prematurely asserted both ideas fit the budget despite missing inputs. The specialized C-S-2 report also leaves ambiguity about a private mock-up and using restricted notes in a proposed independent activity; it does not prove a legal violation, nor should it be treated as verified permission. Full scores, exact examples, the independent scorer's original results and audit corrections are in [test results](../experiments/09-creative-salvage/test-results.md).

The investigator namespaces hid the project and other temporary workspaces, including the answer key; all inputs were hashed before and after. Other host paths and Codex runtime directories remained visible, the OS user and model service were shared, and event logs are not proof against every possible leakage channel. Evaluators saw anonymous report labels, but writing style could reveal the treatment. The benchmark is synthetic, text-only, narrowly themed and designed by the same team. There is no human rating of usefulness or genuine surprise.

## User evaluation and UGREEN feasibility

**Human testing is pending.** Three unsent anonymized participant packets and a counterbalanced [creator evaluation protocol](../experiments/09-creative-salvage/human-evaluation-protocol.md) are ready. Do not treat the AI reviewers as creators or user validation.

The installed official UGREEN catalog documents file discovery, document reading/comparison, evidence collection and optional photo operations, with parent-to-expert delegation and completion-state handling. A read-only text proposal is conceptually plausible. Actual NAS execution, returned source IDs/excerpts, incomplete-coverage behavior, multimodal asset/rights matching and follow-up decision persistence remain unverified. No NAS agent was called; no visual, editing, publication, background service or persistent index was demonstrated. See [capability audit](../experiments/09-creative-salvage/capability-audit.md).

## Three-minute demonstration, if later verified

A creator authorizes a small fictional archive and asks for a free display using existing materials. The Skill finds written observation notes and a paper-workshop description, proposes a combined display, then shows the exact source clause restricting public use of one pattern. It offers a narrower text-only direction and requests confirmation of venue and materials. Show source paths and distinguish known restriction from unknown rights. This is a **planned** text-only demo, not footage of a running UGREEN Skill. A realistic demo must use actual returned expert evidence and avoid implying legal clearance.

## What would change the decision

A **provisional GO** would require real creators to find at least one grounded proposal meaningfully useful or surprising compared with a competent generic baseline, without critical permission or citation failures, plus a plausible official-agent workflow and no discovered feasibility contradiction. A **STOP/pivot** is justified if creators consistently find it generic, if outputs cannot reliably preserve source/rights distinctions, if the necessary expert evidence cannot be obtained on device, or if a closer competitor offers the same experience without a credible differentiation.

**Next action:** run the prepared counterbalanced creator pilot with 3–5 consenting people, using the same fictional files and anonymized generic/specialized outputs; record “neither” choices and all errors. In parallel only when real device access is available, obtain actual file/document/knowledge expert results to check provenance and failure states. Do not substitute synthetic AI personas for users. A human pilot is an exploratory convenience sample, not a market estimate.

A skeptical judge may conclude this is an ordinary AI brainstorm over files plus familiar rights review. Its best chance is a creator-validated, memorable source-grounded choice that would otherwise be difficult to make.
