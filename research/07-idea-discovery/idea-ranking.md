# Five-candidate comparison

Research date 2026-10-26. The five dimensions have equal **20% internal weights**, not the official contest rubric. Score each 1–5 (5 strongest); total = 4 × sum, maximum 100. This is our subjective, pre-user-test judgment, not calibrated measurement. Real user value reflects reports of the underlying problem, not demonstrated demand for our product. NAS dependency measures practical benefit of an existing archive and registered agents, not exclusivity: a mounted NAS and generic assistant can perform similar work. Feasibility means plausibility under documented read-only delegation, not proven execution. Judge impact is our hypothesis.

| Candidate | Value | Differentiation | NAS fit | Feasibility | Demo | Total | Principal justification |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Knowledge Succession Rehearsal | 4 | 4 | 4 | 4 | 4 | **80** | Repeated self-hosted handoff pain; an active test of whether a novice can act from existing records. Exact product novelty and impact are unproven. Document-only path limits cross-modal risk. |
| Consent Before Reuse | 4 | 3 | 4 | 3 | 5 | **76** | Photographer release friction plus memorable opportunity blocked by source terms. Rights-aware DAM exists; photo-to-contract matching and legal safety are hard. |
| Conversation Bridge | 4 | 2 | 4 | 3 | 5 | **72** | Real context-loss reports and emotionally clear demo. MemoryJam already advertises almost the same photo/context questions; mixed-media handoff unverified. |
| Repair Memory | 4 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 3 | **64** | Coherent household need; HomeMemory and grounded repair assistants closely match it. Safety and model-number matching constrain demo. |
| Surprise Me, current broad formulation | 3 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 4 | **64** | Existing local experiment work supports feasibility of a text simulation but generic Codex matched or surpassed specialized variants; novelty and outcome are diffuse. |

**Why these five?** They cover continuity, creator rights, family memories, household work, and the current baseline. Other concepts remain speculative and are not excluded by a precise mathematical pre-screening. Scores have uncertainty of at least one point per dimension. For instance, if succession differentiation fell from 4 to 2 because a closer competing tool is found, it would score 72 and tie Conversation Bridge; if its actual NAS integration failed, it would fall further. These are preference aids, not a scientifically validated ranking.

## Hard feasibility checks

| Check | Succession rehearsal | Consent/reuse | Conversation bridge | Repair memory | Surprise Me |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Plausible documented expert delegation | Yes, file/document/knowledge | Yes, photo/document/knowledge; linking unverified | Yes, photo/document/knowledge; linking unverified | Yes, file/document/knowledge | Yes, file/document/knowledge |
| Requires unsupported backend, executable or UI for core? | No; chat-based exercise | No for text board; rendered montage excluded | No for text questions; no voice/video generation | No | No |
| Sensitive access beyond bounded permission? | No, but potentially sensitive; selected records only | No, but releases are sensitive | No, but personal images/voice need consent | No, limited records | No, chosen scope |
| Small reproducible dataset and verifiable output? | Yes, fictional notes and expected checklist; real usefulness needs users | Yes, asset ID + grant/restriction | Yes, photo + note + ambiguous control | Yes, manual + service note | Yes, prior fixtures |
| Distinct from generic AI upload? | User-focused scenario and NAS retrieval are proposed workflow advantages, not proved | Cross-media native retrieval plus conservative rights gate, not proved | Existing archive access, but MemoryJam and generic AI close | Native archive access, not proved | Weak distinction; generic Codex already strong |

A yes for documented actions is not a yes for data flow, stable citations, model performance, actual NAS or privacy. The read-only investigation must never expose credentials, determine legal authority, certify disaster recovery, or tell a user to undertake risky repairs. Hardware evidence could reverse the ranking.
