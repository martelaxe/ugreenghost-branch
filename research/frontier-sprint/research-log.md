## When we stopped

Additional general searches for “AI file assistant”, “memory”, and “creative archive” repeatedly rediscover generic assistants or prior Experiments 07–19; stop broad brainstorming and another synthetic text benchmark. Prior-art searching for the three chosen experiences is **not saturated**: this is a limited sample. Most information-rich next work is a tiny same-input comparison around real audio-plus-image inputs and actual catalog return feasibility; a real migration export and a willing user would test the practical wildcard. Hardware inspection, privacy validation, user interviews, licensing, app-level playback and exhaustive patent analysis remain unperformed. We cannot quantify the fraction of the web or product market covered.
