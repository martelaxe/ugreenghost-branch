Cutoff 2026-10-27. Search tactics included alternate terms, primary product docs, GitHub projects/issues and targeted patent searches. This is a quick prior-art review, not a patentability or freedom-to-operate opinion. A patent application describes a claim, not evidence that a product works; publication date and legal status do not establish our freedom to implement it. All demand and differentiation judgments remain hypotheses.

## The negative control

Semantic audio search, transcript-to-player links, multicamera sync, metadata sidecars, context-aware search and event timelines are all existing categories. Combining them in a single product may still help people, but no source establishes exclusive novelty, and no benchmark in this sprint demonstrates the interaction to be better than generic AI or existing apps. The Wild direction is retained for the quality of its observable result, not because its ingredients are new or the current platform supports them.
