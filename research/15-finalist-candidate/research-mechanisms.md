# Three research mechanisms

1. **Metamorphic tests for changing evidence.** [When Knowledge Changes: Metamorphic Testing of RAG Systems with Mutations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.26843), 2026, studies retrieval and generation under corpus changes, defines 11 mutation operators and evaluates >28,000 mutants across five datasets. Its insight is to compare expected response relations after purposeful data changes, not merely score one static answer. Here we can evaluate a *small external local experiment*: a relevant source mutation should change the linked requirement; an irrelevant change should not. The Markdown Skill can compare a user-supplied prior review to current evidence via file/document/knowledge experts. We do not implement the paper's retrieval index/chunking mutations, automated oracle, complete metrics or RAG system; its reported results do not validate our Skill. For an owner, a visible changed requirement could reveal that a previously reviewed package no longer meets its stated purpose. It requires an explicit prior review and manual rerun, not monitoring.
2. **Curated procedure with paired baseline.** [SkillsBench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.12670), v4 June 2026, reports matched curated-Skill/no-Skill testing across 87 tasks and 18 configurations, with aggregate gains and narrower modules outperforming larger bundles in its evaluation. We can encode a compact requirement-evidence procedure and compare G/S on identical tasks. We do not reproduce its benchmark, deterministic verifier or claim its results transfer to UGREEN. A user benefits only if the packaged flow actually makes review easier and reliable.
3. **Trigger, procedural compliance and boundaries.** [Skill-Use](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.04828), August 2026, separates whether an agent triggers a Skill, follows it and avoids forbidden actions; authors evaluate 79 skills and 177 tasks across two harnesses. Here we can explicitly inspect correct expert ownership, no unauthorized writes and truthful UNKNOWN. We have not implemented the benchmark's progressive-disclosure harness or measured autonomous triggering on UGREEN. It emphasizes that a valid file and a useful procedure do not guarantee real-world execution.

Selected: a small **requirement → supporting/contrary evidence → status** review plus an explicit previous-versus-current comparison. The paper's metamorphic technique is primarily used to test our system; the Skill does not generate mutations or certify an archive. This is a structured reasoning contract, not an implementation of a research algorithm or a new data-integrity engine.
