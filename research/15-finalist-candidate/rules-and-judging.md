# Rules and judging — checked 2026-10-27

Authoritative [rules](https://ugreenhackathon.devpost.com/rules), [event requirements](https://ugreenhackathon.devpost.com/) and [official starter kit](https://ai.ugreen.com/pages/hackathon-starter-kit): Round 1 closes **November 15, 2026, 11:45 p.m. Pacific**. It requires a separate proposal PDF answering ten questions in no more than 500 English words total and a pre-test Skill ZIP. The event requirements state PDF ≤1 MB; ZIP and uncompressed package each ≤10 MB, each file ≤1 MB, at most 100 files. Main entry `SKILL.md`; only supported directly linked Markdown references; no scripts, binaries, manifest or arbitrary runtime. Disclose AI tools and their principal uses, third-party components, sources and licenses where applicable. No device test or video is required in Round 1.

Finalists receive one assigned remote testing day; Round 2 must use the same project, successfully run on the assigned NAS and show actual device footage. Rules say video should be less than three minutes and judges need not watch beyond three minutes; aim under three. Up to 30 finalists; no runtime evidence alone should penalize Round 1. Check eligibility and latest official rules before entry.

Four equal judging criteria: User Value, Innovation & Differentiation, Technical Quality, AI NAS Fit & Validation. Most Creative prizes use Innovation & Differentiation under the rules' prize allocation, not a prediction of a win. No knowledge of private judge preferences is claimed.

Public entries with overlap include [Publish-Safe Photo Review](https://devpost.com/software/publish-safe-photo-review) (classifies a photo set with uncertain cases and approved album/report) and [ClaimReady Home Inventory](https://devpost.com/software/claimready-home-inventory) (source evidence, gaps and claim pack). Both report no NAS runtime yet. The full gallery/SkillHub was not comprehensively indexed; this is not proof of novelty or an accusation of copying.
