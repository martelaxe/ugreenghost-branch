# Competitor reality check

Reviewed 2026-10-26. Product pages document features, not our independent tests. “?” means this exact behavior was not established by the cited material, **not** that the product cannot do it. Comparison concerns the complete Missing Piece flow: requested task → evidence expectation → authorized candidate search → compare versions → user decision → revised, cited result. General assistants can be prompted to perform much of it; the local benchmark confirms that for simple text fixtures.

Matrix keys: **T** file task; **M** identify missing input; **E** expected vs not required; **R** recover in another authorized location; **C** conflicts/versions; **H** user decision before proceeding; **P** evidence/limitations; **D** decision persists in next output; **Chat** ordinary conversation. `Y` = documented for the stated scope or directly observed locally; `~` = related capability documented but full behavior not verified; `?` = unknown from reviewed sources. No cell is a blanket claim of product impossibility.

The official [UGREEN gallery](https://ugreenhackathon.devpost.com/project-gallery) was not published when reviewed; the public ClaimReady page can still be found separately. [UGREEN Skills Hub](https://skills.ugreen.com/skill-hub/) listings were inaccessible during prior work and not exhaustively surveyed. Therefore existing Skills and submissions cannot be ruled out.

**Reality check:** G completed all four synthetic tasks, including a conditional report and the false-gap control. A well-instructed generic assistant with the same NAS-folder access may already offer the entire proposed Missing Piece experience. A reusable skill, bounded scope and reliable evidence handoff are plausible workflow packaging advantages, but none was shown to outperform or reduce effort for a real user. An invoice-only exception workflow has particularly close direct competitors. For Creative Salvage, the possible differentiator is the *choice of a usable new creative direction tied to original assets and restrictions*, not the claim that generic models or DAM products cannot do it. It remains untested against them.
