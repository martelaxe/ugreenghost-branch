# Focused frontier research: practical implications

Research checked 2026-10-26; papers and vendor claims are not our independent validation. We do not assume the hackathon Skill can run their training code, persistent stores, shell scripts or autonomous optimization. Examples are research context, not an instruction to copy implementation.

1. **Skills externalize procedure, not model weights.** [Google Research's 2026 Agent Skills survey](https://research.google/pubs/a-survey-on-agent-skills-externalized-procedural-knowledge-in-language-models/) frames Skills as reusable procedural knowledge and identifies benchmark realism and continual learning as open issues. [OpenAI's official Skills guide](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/tools-skills) describes a discoverable manifest and reusable instructions/supporting files. Their execution formats differ from UGREEN's Markdown-only challenge; do not import OpenAI shell or executable support into UGREEN.
2. **Bounded edits plus held-out validation can outperform unchecked self-rewriting in a study.** [Microsoft Research SkillOpt, June 30 2026](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/blog/skillopt-agent-skills-as-trainable-parameters/) reports optimizing bounded textual edits against benchmarks with a frozen target model and a validation gate. This is the authors' result on their tasks; our Experiment 05/09 results do not validate it for NAS. No autonomous self-editing or model training is documented here. An offline optimizer could change instructions, not the model's weights; avoid training/test leakage.
3. **Skill effectiveness is task-dependent.** [SkillsBench, February 2026](https://www.skillsbench.ai/skillsbench.pdf) evaluates agents with and without curated task skills using executable checks. It motivates task-specific held-out comparisons rather than assuming longer or shorter instructions are universally better. Our tests remain a few synthetic text cases, not the benchmark.
4. **Evolving playbooks are an active research direction, with safety costs.** [Agentic Context Engineering, October 2025](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.04618) reports generation, reflection and curation to update contextual strategies; claimed gains are authors' benchmark results. Continuous or persistent self-improvement is not an established UGOS Skill feature. For our MVP, no memory or self-modification is assumed.
5. **Personal retrieval is a real failure mode.** [PersonaBench, February 2025](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.20616) uses simulated private data and reports difficulty retrieving personal information. An agent missing a reference cannot prove no dependency exists; incomplete coverage and identity resolution matter. Synthetic results do not quantify UGREEN accuracy.
6. **Multimodal personal context is possible in research but not a device guarantee.** [OmniQuery, CHI 2025](https://doi.org/10.1145/3706598.3713448) explores complex questions over captured multimodal memories. UGREEN documents photo and audio actions, but their formats, privacy settings and evidence propagation remain unverified; omit them from the minimum demo.
7. **Competent tool use still needs bounded authority and explicit errors.** [UGREEN's official Starter Kit](https://ai.ugreen.com/pages/hackathon-starter-kit) requires using actual prior outputs, confirmation for mutations and clear empty-result handling. A capable model cannot infer a user's authorization or file's existence from absent search hits. These are a product contract, not a claim that Markdown enforces deterministic security.

**Research answers:** There is no evidence here that detailed instructions become universally obsolete as models improve. Essential boundaries—scope, user approval, untrusted source handling, provenance and failure reporting—are independent of model cleverness. A contract may improve reliability but remains probabilistic in a model-mediated workflow; our deterministic previous harness tests did not validate NAS enforcement. Procedure generation/reuse is possible in principle, but safe automatic reuse needs independent evaluation, versioning and authority not established in the kit. We omit self-optimization from the MVP: it is secondary to proving the actual dependency preview and requires an evaluation system not available inside the Skill.
