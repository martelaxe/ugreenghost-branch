# Three competing visions and selection

Scores are subjective 1–5 assessments of the current **proposed MVP**, not user ratings or official results. Four equally weighted dimensions follow the [UGREEN judging rubric](https://ugreenhackathon.devpost.com/rules); total = 5 × their sum, out of 100. WOW, risk and differentiation are separate; risk 5 means more risk. All are uncertain by at least one point. Runtime and real users are unverified, so no concept has earned an unconditional GO.

| Vision | User value | Innovation | Technical quality | AI NAS fit & validation | Total | WOW | Risk | Honest prototype effort | Difference from built-in assistant |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|---|
| Impact Rehearsal | 4 | 3 | 3 | 4 | **70** | 4 | 4 | Small Markdown Skill plus text fixtures; real NAS provenance remains a major dependency | 2/5: built-in baseline untested; generic Codex already produced similar result. |
| Rebuild Kit | 4 | 2 | 4 | 3 | **65** | 3 | 3 | Small text artifact from read-only records; actual device still untested | 1/5: generic agent already made the handout; creative and DAM products overlap. |
| Negotiation Rehearsal | 3 | 3 | 3 | 3 | **60** | 3 | 4 | Text-only prototype plausible; realistic user evaluation and safeguards still needed | 1/5: standard chat role-play can likely do it; no local comparison was run. |

## 1. Impact Rehearsal — selected

**Vision (speculative):** An owner points to a file, folder or proposed change and sees an evidence-linked, cross-application dependency map before action. A mature system might inspect more formats, permissions, app-native references and reversible plans. The installed kit does not establish those future capabilities.

**MVP:** “Before I move this file, what might be affected?” Within selected authorized folders, file/document/knowledge experts are asked to find actual records, inspect path references and contradictory context, and return real evidence. The parent writes a small impact map, explicitly separates direct references, inferred effects and unknowns, then advises a review step. It performs no move. No guarantee of complete references, real app breakage or undo. A 60-second reveal could show a seemingly unused cover connected to two project notes and an unchecked external partner.

**Evidence:** A fresh [local probe](../../experiments/11-ai-native-nas/concept-probes/outputs/impact/raw-response.md) found both direct references and the unknown partner in fictional records. Generic Codex did this without the Skill, so the innovation is a proposed repeated workflow and visible outcome, not a proven smarter model. [Premiere Link Media](https://helpx.adobe.com/premiere/desktop/organize-media/file-organization/locate-and-link-offline-files.html), [VS Code refactor preview](https://code.visualstudio.com/docs/editing/refactoring) and Obsidian link tools already cover similar outcomes inside their domains.

**Hard checks:** All core operations map to documented read actions; no custom service or mutation is required. NAS data adds context only when actually accessible, not uniquely versus a mounted folder. A textual impact map is more than a summary if it changes a specific proposed action, but its appeal is unvalidated. Reject if real experts cannot preserve source paths, if it claims a move is safe from partial coverage, or if users prefer existing tools.

## 2. Rebuild Kit — fallback

**Vision (speculative):** Turn a user's paused projects into complete, usable assets across formats, with resource and rights checks. Mature rendering and saved workflows are not documented for the Skill.

**MVP:** Read a selected fictional project and produce a four-panel text-only educational handout with source links and missing prerequisites. A fresh [local probe](../../experiments/11-ai-native-nas/concept-probes/outputs/rebuild/raw-response.md) produced the full draft, excluded the absent photo and did not claim venue or printer availability. This is a real text artifact but no printed item, real creator validation or NAS result.

**Hard checks:** File/document/knowledge actions support a plausible text attempt; rights interpretation and evidence handoff remain uncertain. Generic Codex already produced the artifact, and [Canva Magic Write](https://www.canva.com/help/about-magic-write/) and creative asset tools overlap. Reject the stronger originality pitch unless real creators find the experience distinct and useful.

## 3. Negotiation Rehearsal — distinct new direction

**Vision (speculative):** A private owner-authorized archive helps a user practice a negotiation, offering evidence-backed facts and explicitly hypothetical counterpart responses, without contacting anyone.

**MVP:** A one-shot fictional practice script with actual contract excerpts, a user-provided aim and uncertain assumptions. A generic AI may already do this, and there is no fresh local probe or independent user demand in this experiment. Legal advice and inferred counterpart intent are excluded.

**Hard checks:** A bounded document-only version is plausible in catalog, but personal commercial records are sensitive and the meaningful NAS dependency is weak. No unverified persistent role-play state, autonomous sending or legal clearance. It remains a research candidate rather than a fallback winner.

**Decision:** choose exactly one primary, Impact Rehearsal, and one fallback, Rebuild Kit. Do not combine them as a single Skill. The primary is a credible, practical design hypothesis but not yet an exceptional verified experience; if device or user testing fails, redesign rather than presenting synthetic output as proof.
