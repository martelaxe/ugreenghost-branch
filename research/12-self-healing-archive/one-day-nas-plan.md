# One-day NAS plan

Round 1 proposal and initial Skill deadline: [November 15, 2026, 11:45 p.m. Pacific](https://ugreenhackathon.devpost.com/rules), subject to official rules. A finalist may receive an assigned 24-hour remote test window; check actual invitation and access arrangements, not an assumed slot.

**Before access:** freeze `prototype/project-rescue/SKILL.md` and checker output/hash; prepare a clean disposable 26-file archive and expected result, two identical and one different candidate; confirm authorized paths and exact prompt. Prepare screenshots and independent hashes, a temporary destination directory, approval and failure checks; do not mix real private files. Prepare a read-only fallback. Obtain official interface instructions.

**Suggested 24-hour priorities:** first 2 hours install/recognize Skill and list files; inspect actual file/document child states, source paths and coverage. Next 3–4 hours run read-only reference and hash check, limited-scope negative test, and collision test. Next 3–4 hours present one exact action and inspect both explicit owner and gateway approval. Only if the runtime can prove effective no-overwrite, source/destination scope, copy semantics and authorized approval: execute one bounded copy on the disposable fixture. Capture separately: approval state, completion, actual source/target listings and hashes, and evidence of preserved shared reference. Spend 2 hours on denial/pending/failure and unavailable verification, if feasible. Reserve ample time to rerun from clean baseline, record actual UI and a three-minute video; if capabilities fail, debug only bounded issues and stop safely.

**Fallbacks:** Missing references or incomplete listing → report unknown and request narrower known files, do not claim coverage. Missing action or uncertain no-overwrite → read-only Impact Rehearsal, accurately label it as a different result. Approval handoff or waiting cannot resume → stop and show pending, no forced copy. Failure/readback mismatch → report unverified or failed state; no automatic retry/rollback. Inability to record device actions → disclose limitation rather than stage it as NAS operation. Keep baseline/snapshots of synthetic test data; these are not UGREEN rollback guarantees.
