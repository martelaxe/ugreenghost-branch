# Candidate selection

Scores are subjective internal judgments out of 5 per official equally weighted dimension, not judges' ratings or a measured competition result. The [rules](https://ugreenhackathon.devpost.com/rules) name User Value, Innovation & Differentiation, Technical Quality, and AI NAS Fit & Validation; validate exact language at submission.

| Candidate | Value | Innovation | Technical | NAS fit | Sum/20 | Reason |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Project Rescue | 4 | 3 | 3 | 4 | 14 | One visible protected copy; close to app relinking; atomic/approval behavior unknown. |
| Context Rescue | 3 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 12 | Useful grouping but wrong context and generic file organization are serious concerns. |
| Album Assembly | 3 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 12 | Visible result but built-in photo products already offer similar functions. |

Separate qualitative assessments: Project Rescue has stronger before/after and emotional payoff, medium WOW and medium simplicity; deterministic fixture test possible, but no documented guaranteed atomic no-overwrite or undo and NAS approval untested. Context Rescue is also an actionable copy, but the reason to associate files can be ambiguous. Album assembly may be a cleaner sanctioned workflow, but it is familiar and its album/member semantics are untested. These subjective scores are not fine-grained evidence.

**Primary:** Project Rescue, restricted to one strongly evidenced file and an already existing authorized destination. **Fallback:** an honest read-only Impact Rehearsal if safe copy cannot be guaranteed; it is a different deliverable and must never be presented as the action succeeding. Album creation is a separate speculative product concept, not an additional fallback recommendation. No case should delete merely to produce a dramatic demo. Round 1 suitability is a proposal decision, not a device certification.
