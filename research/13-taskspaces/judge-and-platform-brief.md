# Judge and platform brief

Evidence checked 2026-10-26: [official rules](https://ugreenhackathon.devpost.com/rules), [starter kit](https://ai.ugreen.com/pages/hackathon-starter-kit), [UGOS](https://ai.ugreen.com/pages/operating-system), [Model Manager notice](https://ai.ugreen.com/pages/notice-on-model-manager), installed Uliya catalog and prior [Experiment 12 audit](../12-self-healing-archive/action-capability-audit.md).

The four official criteria carry equal weight: **User Value** (clear problem/workflow, useful feedback and meaningful result); **Innovation & Differentiation** (substantive new function, interaction or workflow versus conventional AI and existing solutions); **Technical Quality** (sound expert orchestration, permissions, errors and appropriate reliability); **AI NAS Fit & Validation** (meaningful use of storage, data, computing, privacy or supported capabilities). Most Creative is determined by Innovation & Differentiation, subject to the rules' prize allocation. These are not private judge preferences.

Round 1: PDF answers ten questions in at most 500 English words, initial Markdown Skill ZIP; no device evidence required, submission due November 15, 2026 at 11:45 p.m. Pacific. Round 2 finalists receive one assigned device day November 22–28 and must submit the same project working on UGREEN and video footage. Live local/hosted models both allowed; privacy depends on actual selected configuration. Initial static validation does not prove hardware works.

UGREEN already describes search/indexing, document summaries, model management and file management. A text summary, AI search or file copy by itself is ordinary product behavior, not automatically a novel platform extension. A possible extension is a concise task-specific ready-to-use artifact that links evidence, records key decisions and exposes safe approved actions—provided users actually value it and it works with registered experts. No custom interface, hidden long-lived state, UGREEN roadmap or judge preference is evidenced.
