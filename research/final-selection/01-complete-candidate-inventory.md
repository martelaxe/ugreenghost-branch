The earlier decisions, research and prototypes remain the primary record. Scores in the companion file are subjective design judgments. D = documented expert fragments; C = conditional on actual returns/approval; B = blocked as conceived. A generic agent may reproduce all of these; a reusable instruction is not a new tool.
