Original user's September 24/25 history question deserves a concrete correction: [File Version Explorer](https://support.ugnas.com/detail/article/en-US/316) displays retained versions and folders where enabled; the documented default is eight versions and maximum 32, not an unlimited time machine or guarantee that every past move is reversible. Its existence reduces the novelty of simple version-history proposals without disproving richer historical interfaces.
