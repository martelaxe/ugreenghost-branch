# Twelve possible WOW moments

These are proposed interactions, not verified NAS features or evidence of user interest. “Beyond search” describes the *intended user-visible outcome*, not a claim generic AI cannot do it. F = `file-agent`, D = `document-agent`, K = `knowledge-agent`, P = `photo-agent`; parent synthesis and any multi-turn behavior are unverified. All work is scoped to explicitly authorized sources; optional saving requires approval. Existing-product comparisons are bounded by [competitor check](competitor-check.md). Four finalists marked ★ are intentionally different; Archive Quest is a new direction relative to Experiments 01–09.

## ★ 1. The $420 breadcrumb — Digital Archaeologist

**User types:** “Investigate my archive for an overlooked financial issue and draft a message I can use.” **Looks like:** ordinary records retrieval. **Reveal:** a contract and payment trail suggest a deposit may not have been applied. **Why it matters / memorable:** a concrete possible credit becomes a short ready-to-review message; a user sees why the amount remains unconfirmed. **Existing software:** expense/reconciliation systems and AI agents match receipts/invoices; generic Codex already discovered similar discrepancies in experiment 01. **UGREEN:** F/D/K. **Beyond search:** a qualified cross-record conclusion and action draft, still replicable with a capable generic agent.

## ★ 2. Pocket Exhibit — Archive Alchemy

**User types:** “Turn my old projects into a six-panel educational zine using materials I can reuse.” **Looks like:** reading forgotten notes. **Reveal:** a fully written miniature zine combines a field diary and paper-workshop idea. **Why it matters / memorable:** the output is something to edit or print, not a list of ideas. **Existing software:** AI document creation, Adobe asset tools and creative writing assistants can make comparable drafts. **UGREEN:** F/D/K, with no assumed rendering or print control. **Beyond search:** a complete source-grounded text artifact with rights and unknowns, not proof of a unique technical capability.

## ★ 3. Fork in the Budget — Alternate Reality Machine

**User types:** “Show me the two possible budgets before I decide which quote to accept.” **Looks like:** document comparison. **Reveal:** the decision changes a project from under to over budget. **Why it matters / memorable:** a visible fork produces two usable plans and an explicit choice. **Existing software:** spreadsheet scenarios and AI assistants already do conditional analysis; Experiment 08 generic Codex did it. **UGREEN:** F/D/K. **Beyond search:** a decision artifact showing consequences, with no claim that only NAS can calculate them.

## ★ 4. Archive Quest — the files become a game

**User types:** “Turn my workshop archive into a playable mystery for my family.” **Looks like:** browsing old notes. **Reveal:** the user solves a two-step mystery from a dated ticket, a color change and a stock card. **Why it matters / memorable:** an ordinary archive becomes an activity people can play together, potentially prompting a conversation. **Existing software:** [Escaply](https://www.escaply.com/en) already advertises escape rooms from source documents; [Family Game Night](https://familygamenight.app/) advertises personalized games. This is not global novelty. **UGREEN:** F/D/K; possible photo use is optional and unverified. **Beyond search:** a playable text puzzle with an answer and evidence; a generic assistant can also generate it. No real lock, persistent game engine or live reply validation is assumed.

## 5. Permission Mirror

**User types:** “Could I exhibit this old photograph?” **Looks like:** finding a photo. **Reveal:** the same image can be used privately but public exhibition needs consent, according to a specific document. **Why it matters / memorable:** a seductive asset changes status when the user sees the clause. **Existing software:** rights-aware DAM already does this. **UGREEN:** F/D/K and optionally P. **Beyond search:** a concrete hold-or-review decision, not a legal clearance.

## 6. The Vanishing Page

**User types:** “Prepare my scan collection for review; what should I check first?” **Looks like:** counting files. **Reveal:** an explicitly stated document manifest mentions a page not located in the reviewed scan. **Why it matters / memorable:** a user can find the original before discarding it. **Existing software:** page counts and scan-validation tools exist; no exhaustive comparison was done. **UGREEN:** F/D/K; PDF page-count support is cataloged but source format/coverage untested. **Beyond search:** a human-check task, not proof a missing page does not exist.

## 7. Successor Challenge

**User types:** “Test whether my colleague can use my handoff notes.” **Looks like:** summarizing a directory. **Reveal:** a colleague tries one task and discovers an undocumented handoff step. **Why it matters / memorable:** someone sees where a real transfer might fail. **Existing software:** digital legacy products and generic assistants cover parts; Experiment 07 explored it. **UGREEN:** F/D/K. **Beyond search:** a practice exercise with explicit unknowns, no guarantee of system recovery.

## 8. Repair Detective

**User types:** “Prepare my technician for this appliance's recurring problem.” **Looks like:** retrieving a manual. **Reveal:** a service note for the correct model points to an earlier repair, while a similar wrong-model manual is excluded. **Why it matters / memorable:** a concrete handoff may save diagnosis time. **Existing software:** HomeMemory and manual-grounded assistants are close competitors. **UGREEN:** F/D/K. **Beyond search:** a cited, model-specific handoff, not DIY repair instruction or proof of time saved.

## 9. The Question a Photo Cannot Answer

**User types:** “What should I ask my family about this old photograph?” **Looks like:** inspecting a photo. **Reveal:** a note contradicts the date, leading to a specific open question rather than a fabricated story. **Why it matters / memorable:** a real relative supplies the missing answer. **Existing software:** MemoryJam already advertises personalized photo/context questions. **UGREEN:** P/D/K; cross-modal provenance unverified. **Beyond search:** a prompt for a real conversation, not unique photo AI.

## 10. The Version That Changed Everything

**User types:** “Why do these two project plans disagree?” **Looks like:** comparing versions. **Reveal:** a rejected change explains the conflicting amounts. **Why it matters / memorable:** the user sees a sourced decision boundary rather than choosing the newest-looking filename. **Existing software:** version comparison and generic AI reconciliation exist. **UGREEN:** F/D/K. **Beyond search:** compare consequences and request confirmation, without claiming to know anyone's motive.

## 11. The Exhibition With a Hole

**User types:** “Make a short source-backed exhibition outline for this collection.” **Looks like:** finding images and captions. **Reveal:** one requested panel is explicitly missing, so the outline contains a visible question for a curator. **Why it matters / memorable:** the empty space becomes a task instead of an invented historical detail. **Existing software:** exhibition planning and source-grounded assistants already cover parts. **UGREEN:** F/D/K; optional P. **Beyond search:** a complete textual outline with an honest gap; no generated gallery UI.

## 12. The Asset's Second Job

**User types:** “What safe new project could I make from these abandoned materials?” **Looks like:** inventory. **Reveal:** material from one unused project fits another's present need, but a rights clause blocks one tempting component. **Why it matters / memorable:** the final idea changes to a narrower feasible alternative. **Existing software:** Adobe, Contesimal and generic Codex overlap; Experiment 09 did not show a reliable specialized advantage. **UGREEN:** F/D/K. **Beyond search:** a cited, actionable proposal, with novelty and real creator appeal unproven.
