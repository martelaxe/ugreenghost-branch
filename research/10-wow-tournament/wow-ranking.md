# Tournament ranking: user-facing judgment, not user feedback

One model-generated artifact per candidate, evaluated by the experiment author on 2026-10-26. Scores 1–5 are **subjective hypotheses**, not judge, participant, market or AI-generated user feedback. The five scores describe the particular prototype and plausible application; uncertainty is at least ±1 point and not statistically meaningful. Surprise and memorability cannot be measured without actual people. NAS relevance is potential integration with a selected archive, not an exclusive ability. Technical plausibility comes from cataloged building blocks, not verified device execution.

**Primary: Archive Quest, exploratory only.** It is the most different visible interaction in this tournament and could make personal storage feel emotionally engaging rather than merely useful. It is **not yet an exceptional demo**: the actual puzzle is simple and close competitors already offer source-based games. The choice weights the requested WOW/memorability above the unweighted total; the two 18/25 practical concepts could be stronger if users prefer utility. A better real-archive game and user preference test could overturn this decision.

**Fallback: Pocket Exhibit.** It produces a complete, reviewable textual artifact and appropriately limits photo reuse. Generic AI and design tools can do comparable work; it should not be pitched as unique. Do not merge the two finalists merely to add features.

## Separate evaluation and hard checks

### Archive Quest
- **WOW / useful result:** a playable two-step text handout with source-backed solution; possible family conversation. Whether anyone finds this fun is unknown.
- **Original enough?** New within our project, **not novel as a general category**: Escaply and source-based quizzes already exist. Generic Codex produced the entire artifact.
- **NAS reason:** chosen personal documents can supply personally meaningful clues, if users authorize them. A mounted archive or upload works for another AI too.
- **Failures / unverified:** two non-verbatim quotations, visible same-response spoiler, weak riddle, unverified photo input, expert provenance and follow-up state. No game engine or custom UI demonstrated.
- **Kill test:** five real participants fail to solve a revised game, say none is engaging, or sources cannot safely produce correct solvable clues. Do not assume five people prove broad demand.

### Pocket Exhibit
- **WOW / useful result:** six labeled, copy-ready text panels from notes plus newly written verse; not an actual printed or designed zine.
- **Original enough?** Existing creative AI and asset tools overlap; generic Codex did the work.
- **NAS reason:** source notes and terms live together; same data can be uploaded/mounted elsewhere.
- **Failures / unverified:** citations are text paths rather than clickable references, reuse permissions and completeness are not guaranteed on actual experts, and creative merit is unvalidated.
- **Kill test:** actual creators find the panels generic or unsupported; real agent outputs lack retrievable source references.

### Digital Archaeologist
- **WOW / useful result:** source-backed potential $420 issue and a draft inquiry, with no assertion a refund is due.
- **Original enough?** Generic Codex and reconciliation products cover similar results; the amount can be memorable, not a novel mechanism.
- **NAS reason:** established records in an authorized archive can be consulted. Generic mounted-folder agents can do likewise.
- **Failures / unverified:** unrecorded refund/adjustment, inaccessible banking records, false payment matching, and no live NAS return provenance.
- **Kill test:** the source trail is insufficient or later evidence shows the inference was already resolved; must avoid asserting debt or sending messages.

### Fork in the Budget
- **WOW / useful result:** two conditional draft budgets and one decision; arithmetic correct for the fixture.
- **Original enough?** Excel scenarios and generic assistants already do this; data-driven choice is useful but not especially distinctive.
- **NAS reason:** documents may be scattered in shared storage; nothing shown is NAS-exclusive.
- **Failures / unverified:** quote not accepted, incomplete cost coverage, real expert comparison and state retention.
- **Kill test:** decision has no meaningful consequence, unreliable amounts or unsupported acceptance claims.

Across all four, reviewer-checked path existence and caveats appear in [evidence checks](../../experiments/10-wow-tournament/evidence-checks.md). The captured local report paths are workspace-relative text, not portable clickable citations. No input files were modified and no network sending was done.
