# Missing primitives and what the Skill cannot fake

**Future primitive: `interact-under-policy(object, recipient, operation, scope)`**. A trusted broker authorizes one operation over selected private content without granting unrestricted file read, returns a bounded result plus a non-sensitive receipt, and permits the owner to revoke *future* operations. It needs strong subject authentication, protected data access, isolated model/tool execution, robust output restrictions, audit, multiple-owner policy, race-safe policy checks and a stated threat model. It cannot remove knowledge already released, prevent screenshots, guarantee no inference from allowed answers, or be made safe by a `SKILL.md` instruction. The local toy demonstrates a direct bypass. Tahoe capabilities, clean rooms and zero-trust policies are substantial prior art; do not market this as newly invented access control.

**Future primitive: `project(story-beats, exclusion, source-set)`**. A view computes eligible role-preserving alternatives under a constraint, displays gaps and source dependencies, and updates when underlying evidence or authorization changes. A real implementation requires reliable structured metadata, versioned provenance, dependency tracking, stable view identities, explicit change triggers, and a safe render/publish layer. The local SVG generator implements only fixed labels and deterministic selection. `invention-17/SKILL.md` implements an on-demand *instructional proposal*, not a live projection or UI. Current UGREEN documentation does not establish immutable album ordering, reactive refresh, policy enforcement or deletions from past exports.
