# Two different evaluations

These are author judgments, not judges' scores or winning probabilities. They separate invention potential from UGREEN hackathon fit; we have no user tests.

## 1. Invention potential (feasibility deliberately not dominant)

| Concept | Surprise / distinct operation | Prior-art pressure | Judgment |
|---|---|---|---|
| Consent-Bearing Object (W) | High: one stored object offers a narrow interaction and later refuses it | Very high: capabilities, clean rooms, zero-trust and DRM already exist | Most exciting direction as a personal AI storage experience; novelty is the integration and interaction, not the security primitive. Requires a real enforcement design. |
| What-If Exhibit (B) | Medium-high: a visible artifact retains intended roles when a constraint changes; unsupported roles become gaps | High: reactive builds, photo replacement, albums and UGREEN photo entries | An interesting consumer composition of known mechanisms; our toy is too simple to establish semantic creativity. |
| One-Bit Workshop (S) | Medium-low: sources become a branching interaction | Very high: AI tutoring and value-of-information clarification | A reasonable interaction, not a new category; the model test supports only one example. |
| Alternative Past Room | High: inspect a decision with evidence as-of a time | Very high: bitemporal databases and version-control time travel | Interesting only with authentic availability timestamps and a distinct experience. |
| Forgetting That Leaves a Hole | High: absence remains visible after removal | High: archival redaction and tombstones | Strong design tension; a visible gap can itself leak sensitive existence. |
| Skill Quarantine | Medium: untrusted procedures must earn promotion | Very high: skill validation, CI and sandboxing | Useful governance, not a new invention. |

## 2. Hackathon potential, using the official equally weighted categories

| Candidate | User Value | Innovation & Differentiation | Technical Quality | AI NAS Fit & Validation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| W | Possible but no human validation | Distinct interaction; close security prior art | Not achievable as described through Markdown-only delegation | Future concept only; no documented policy enforcement/secure broker |
| B | Useful if substitutions preserve meaning, untested | Visible constraint-and-gap behavior; generic/contest overlap | Local deterministic toy works; actual meaning, identifiers, ordering and album creation unverified | File/document/photo agents plausibly support an on-demand fragment; no reactive filesystem or NAS proof |
| S | Small but understandable | Heavily overlaps existing assistants and active-question methods | One local two-turn sample, no baseline; interactive state unverified | File/document experts plausibly supply authorized notes |

**Best bridge:** B, but it is a research candidate, not submission-ready proof of creativity or device feasibility. **Best skill-native mechanism:** S's interactive branching is plausible, but low originality. **Best wild invention:** W, where the product experience could merit research even if its security ingredients are established. A real comparison must show a meaningful visual substitution with independently assessed semantic roles and a generic baseline; a NAS test must first establish agent identifiers and album semantics. Source: [official judging criteria](https://ugreenhackathon.devpost.com/rules).
